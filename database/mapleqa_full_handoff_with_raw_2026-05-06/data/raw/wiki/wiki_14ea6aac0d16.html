The following is a list of important characters in MapleStory.

Main Characters

Character name Description
World Tree
(Alicia) 		Current Transcendent of Life of Maple World

Shares the role of Transcendent of Life with Gerand Darmoor upon the fusion of Maple World and Grandis.
Creator of the Tower of Oz

Scattered her life force across Maple World to restore the damage brought upon by Damien

Black Mage
(White Mage) 		Former Transcendent of Light of Maple World and temporarily the Transcendent of Light of Grandis

Awakened with the power of destruction by casting out his light, which became Luminous.
Antagonist of Maple World
Founder of Aurora
Created Arcane River

Killed by the Adversary in accordance to his plan to liberate the world from the Overseers.

Rhinne Former Transcendent of Time of Maple World
Goddess of Time

Had her powers stolen by the Black Mage and was trapped in Mirror World

Succeeded as the Transcendent of Time by her children, Alpha and Beta.

Zero
(Alpha and Beta) 		Current Transcendent of Time of Maple World

Shares the role of Transcendent of Time with Chronica upon the fusion of Maple World and Grandis.
Child of Rhinne, split into Alpha and Beta

Was trapped in Mirror World by the Black Mage and Will before they broke free.
Alpha and Beta wield the weapons Lazuli and Lapis respectively

Cygnus Current Empress of Maple World
Aria's descendant
Leader of the Cygnus Knights and the Maple Alliance.
Protected by Shinsoo
Partially awakened as a reserve to an unknown Transcendent of Maple World
Aeona

(Tana)

Transcendent of Light of Grandis and later Maple World upon the fusion of the two worlds.

Once held the title of Tana, the reserve to the Transcendent of Light, and holds the power of creation.

Gained the title of Aeona upon killing her sister, who held the power of destruction.

Had her powers temporarily stolen by the Black Mage until his death.

Gerand Darmoor

(Aaron)

Current Transcendent of Life of Grandis

Shares the role of Transcendent of Time with Alicia upon the fusion of Maple World and Grandis.
Antagonist of Grandis
God-King of the High Flora

Seeks to destroy all life and recreate it in his image.

Chronica Current Transcendent of Time of Grandis

Shares the role of Transcendent of Time with Zero upon the fusion of Maple World and Grandis

Had their powers stolen by Gerand Darmoor and is trapped in the Chronica Sanctuary

Adele

(Adelaide)

Former Knight of the Einherjar

Defender of Gerand Darmoor's father the former God-King

Pledged loyalty to Jerome, the last prince of Ristonia

Rival of Veronica

Afrien King of the Onyx Dragons
Mir's father

Creator of Karote

Idol of the Battlefield

Aran One of the six Heroes of Maple.

Polearm warrior who wields the legendary weapon, Maha.

Ark A former High Flora soldier in Darmoor's army.
Betrayed Darmoor's command after objecting to the war leading to the massacre of various races.

Became a partial Specter after the ritual to forcibly turn him into one was interrupted.

Arkarium Deceased Commander of the Black Mage

Former Guardian of Time

Escorted Tana through the Arcane River, but was stopped by the Adversary.

Killed by the echo of the Black Mage in Morass.
