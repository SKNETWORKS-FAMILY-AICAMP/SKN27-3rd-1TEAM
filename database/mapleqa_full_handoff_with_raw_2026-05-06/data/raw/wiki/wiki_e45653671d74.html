Party Quest

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Party Quests are a type of dungeon / mission with a specific objective that can be attempted individually or a party, depending on the Party Quest in question. Party Quests provide players with EXP and rewards, including special equipment that count toward the Party Quest Set. Below is a list of Party Quests which are available, sorted in ascending order by Level requirements.

Cross World Party Quests

Some Party Quests can only be played through the Cross World Party Quest system, which allows 4 players from any world to come together and face Party Quests exclusive to this system. This unlocks at Level 70, and so all Cross World Party Quests have a level requirement of 70. There is only one specific Party Quests that can be played at one time, which rotates every two weeks. Each Cross World Party Quest entries can be entered 5 times every day.

Upon entering a Cross World Party Quest, Spiegelette will take the player's items for safe keeping and give free potions for the player to use for the duration of the Party Quest.

Upon completion of the Cross World Party Quest, the player will receive Party Points which can be used to buy various items and equipment from the NPC Pub. Note that your points will reset every 8 weeks.

Below is a list of Party Quests which are only available through Cross World Party Quest.

  • Moon Bunny's Rice Cake (Level 70 - 300)
  • First Time Together (Level 70 - 300)
  • Remnant of the Goddess (Level 70 - 300)
  • Forest of Poison Haze (Level 70 - 300)
  • Ariant Coliseum (Level 70 - 300)
  • Wood Theme Park (MapleSEA only) (Level 70 - 300)
