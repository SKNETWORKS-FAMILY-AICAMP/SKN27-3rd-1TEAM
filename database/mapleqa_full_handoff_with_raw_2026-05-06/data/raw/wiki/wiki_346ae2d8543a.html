Dreamer (or GO WEST! in GMS) is an update following New Age. This update includes a new common 6th job skill, Sol Janus, introduces Legion Artifact and quality of life changes including improvements to equipment and character stats.

There are regional differences between the rollout of the update. In Non-KoreaMS servers, the update also includes a job revamp to Angelic Buster[1], which was released before the Dreamer update in KoreaMS, as well as skill improvements for Kanna and Hayato. In ChinaMS and TaiwanMS, the update also includes a job revamp to Mo Xuan.

Original (KoreaMS)

1st: Common Node and Legion Artifact

The first Dreamer update includes a new skill, Sol Janus, as well as introduction of a new Legion Artifact system (Union Artifact in non-GlobalMS servers).

Sol Janus is a 6th job common skill with two modes that the character can choose from when cast: Dusk and Dawn. At a maximum level 30, it grants a passive effect of 100% bonus EXP.

Legion Artifact is a system that replaces Monster Life and is accessed through the Legion System UI. Like the Legion System, it improves player stats for all characters in the world, and can be increased by completing missions and spending Artifact EXP and Artifact Points on Artifacts.

The update also includes:

  • Addition of Equipment, Inner Ability, and Character Presets
    • Character Presets allow the setting of presets for Equipment, Hyper Stats, Inner Ability, Legion, and Link Skills all at once
  • An improved Boss UI to directly enter boss fights, as well as a way to use temporary buffs and potions in practice mode
  • Enabled usage of multiple Nodestones and enhancement of all applicable V Matrix Skills at the same time
  • Burning World and Hyper Burning opened
    • Tera Blink event that allows newly created Hyper Burning characters to quickly reach level 200
  • Dreaming Lachelein event

2nd: Sixth Job Update

  • New Mastery Nodes for every class
  • Cubing generally replaced with an individual potential reset system using mesos
    • Bonus potential cubes remain available until the Milestone Update
  • Daily meso gain from looting limited based on a character's level
  • Removal of Ursus
  • Removal of Intense Power Crystal pricing and meso multiplier in Reboot Worlds

3rd: Epic Dungeon: High Mountain

  • New dungeon available to characters that have advanced to Sixth job, offering EXP and Sol Erda (or Sol Erda Fragments for Reboot World)
  • New Maple Coin system that allows players to exchange coins and Maple Points for mesos
    • Maple Coins are obtained through Epic Dungeon: High Mountain and MAPLE Daily Gift

Non-KoreaMS (Except GlobalMS)

Note: This section uses mostly MapleSEA translations as the other Non-KoreaMS servers, except GlobalMS, use similar translated names in their respective languages.

1st: Angelic Buster Revamp and Epic Dungeon: High Mountain

The revamp of Angelic Buster includes improved appearance, as well as improved graphics and sound for many skills. The Soul Recharge system was removed in favor of MP. Changes were made to her storyline, which affected Kaiser's storyline as well.

Three MVs are released in conjunction with the revamp, Angelic Buster: Shining Heart, Angelic Buster x Lucid: Fly Away, and Angelic Buster: Star Bubble, which are translated into English, Chinese and Japanese in MapleSEA, ChinaMS and JapanMS. Angelic Buster: Shining Heart is also translated into Chinese in TaiwanMS.

The update also includes:

  • Epic Dungeon: High Mountain, offering EXP, Sol Erda Fragments, and Maple Coins
  • Various quality of life improvements
  • Daily meso limit from looting based on a character's level
  • Removal of Ursus
  • Removal of cubes and flame crafting materials from most shops, Monster Collection, Bounty Hunter pouches, and boxes
    • Bosses continue to drop these items
  • Hyper Burning and Tera Blink event
  • Lacheln in a Dream event
  • Angelic Buster Cheer Support event buff[2][3][4][5]

For certain servers as stated below, the update also includes:

  • Removal of Intense Power Crystal pricing and meso multiplier in Reboot Worlds (TaiwanMS and JapanMS only)[6]
  • Removal of Monster Life (MapleSEA only)[7]
  • Potential tier upgrade benefit system for cash cubes (MapleSEA only)[8]
  • Burning World (JapanMS only)[9]
  • Nexon 30th Anniversary event (TaiwanMS only)

The following changes from KoreaMS are not implemented:

  • Cubing system replacement
  • MAPLE Daily Gift reward changes

2nd: Angelic Buster x Lucid

  • Angelic Buster x Lucid event

3rd: Sixth Job Update and Union Artifact

  • New Mastery Nodes for every class
  • Common Core: Sol Janus
  • Union Artifact
    • Additional missions related to the Zipangu and Sengoku questlines, bosses and jobs
  • Sengoku Class Improvements: Kanna and Hayato

For certain servers as stated below, the update also includes:

  • Job Revamp: Mo Xuan (ChinaMS and TaiwanMS only)[10]
  • Additional Legion Artifact missions related to Lynn and Mo Xuan (ChinaMS and TaiwanMS only)[10]
  • Guild Castle changes (ChinaMS and TaiwanMS only)[11]
    • New Personal Research skills, offering Sacred Symbol Selectors, Sol Erda Fragments, and a new map
  • Abyssal Expedition Season 3 - Minar Forest event (MapleSEA only)[12]
  • Maple Expedition - Pharaoh's Treasure Season 2 event (JapanMS only)[13]

GO WEST! (GlobalMS)

1st: Maple Boys

In GlobalMS, the major update is instead named as GO WEST! Inkwell and Dr. Lim are the main characters hosting various exclusive events throughout this update, providing various rewards including exclusive cosmetic items.

The update also includes:

  • Job Revamp: Angelic Buster Redux
  • Epic Dungeon: High Mountain, offering EXP, Sol Erda, and Sol Erda Fragments
  • Legion Artifact
  • Various quality of life improvements
  • Newly obtained Sol Erda Fragments and Nodestones made tradable in Interactive (Regular) worlds
  • Burning World, Hyper Burning, and Tera Blink event
  • Dreaming Lachelein event
  • GO WEST! - Maple Boys exclusive event

The following changes from KoreaMS are not implemented:

  • Cubing system replacement
  • MAPLE Daily Gift reward changes
  • Maple Coin system
  • Daily meso limit
  • Removal of Ursus
  • Intense Power Crystal pricing and meso multiplier changes in Heroic (Reboot) worlds

2nd: Maple Punk

  • New Mastery Nodes for every class
  • Sol Janus Common Node
  • Familiars Update: New Familiar Card drops in Cernium and Burning Cernium
  • Sengoku Class Improvements: Kanna and Hayato
  • Legion Artifact Update: Additional missions related to the Zipangu and Sengoku questlines, bosses and jobs
  • Angelic Buster x Lucid Dream Concert event
  • GO WEST! - Maple Punk exclusive event
  • Maple Expedition - Pharaoh's Treasure Season 2 event[13]

Trivia

  • Server Naming Difference:
    • MSEA/TMS/JMS: Dreamer
    • GMS: GO WEST!
    • CMS: 美妙梦想家 (Wonderful Dreamer, Měi miào mèng xiǎng jiā)
  • On January 3, 2024, Nexon Korea was fined by Korea Fair Trade Commission due to cube rate alterations without informing players[14], which indirectly caused the cubing system replacement in KoreaMS.
  • The GO WEST! themes and promotional art in GlobalMS took inspiration from various Western artists:
    • The first theme and art were inspired by the Pet Shop Boys 1993 cover of Go West.
    • The second theme and art were inspired by Daft Punk, specifically their 2013 single, Get Lucky.
    • The Login BGM was also temporarily changed to the GO WEST! themes in this update.
  • A number of regional differences came across with this update:
    • In ChinaMS and TaiwanMS, Masarayu's Gift Atmospheric Effect that was previously sold in the Ursus shop was moved to the Legion coin shop.
      • In other Non-GlobalMS servers, the effect was moved to the Mileage shop. GlobalMS was not affected as Ursus remained available.
    • In TaiwanMS, a new weekly quest was added, offering a choice of additional Sol Erda or Sol Erda Fragments upon completion of [Daily Quest] Erda's Request 3 times per week.
      • In MapleSEA and JapanMS, this was added in the first Post-Dreamer Update.[15][16]
      • In ChinaMS, this was added in the first part of the Chaser Update.
    • In MapleSEA and JapanMS, 6 additional Sol Erda Fragments were provided upon completion of Erda's Request quest as of this update.
      • This was reversed in the first post-Dreamer Update alongside with the weekly Erda's Gift quest.[15][16]
    • In TaiwanMS, Continental Grandis maps were instanced again, and continue to be instanced as of now.
    • In ChinaMS, the rollout and content for Dreamer were modified as follows:
      • Epic Dungeon: High Mountain was not released upon rollout, and was instead postponed to the second part of the update.
      • Similar to GlobalMS, Maple Coin system was not available, and no Maple Coins were awarded upon completion of Epic Dungeon: High Mountain.
      • Buff Freezers were removed in the third part of the update. In other servers, these were already removed after the Savior Update.
        • Players who owned Battle-Roids were compensated.[17]
