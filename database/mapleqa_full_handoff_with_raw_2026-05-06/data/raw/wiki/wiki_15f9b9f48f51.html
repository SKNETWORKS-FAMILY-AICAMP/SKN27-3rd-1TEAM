Ren or Len is an Anima Warrior released during the MapleStory: Assemble[4] Update.

Once a renowned mercenary known as the Red-Eyed Wanderer, Ren now works as an ordinary adventurer in the Traveler's Guild of Vallora, hoping to buy a special elixir that can heal her grievously injured friend, Saya. However, when a High Flora officer named Lotusha arrives in Vallora, he recognizes Ren as the murderer of his older brother and plans to exact his revenge upon the Red-Eyed Wanderer.

Story

Main article: Ren/Story

Many years ago, a rabbit Anima named Ren worked as a mercenary, using a rare but powerful sword technique called Plum Blossom. One day, Ren received a request from the Vharga in northern Grandis, whose leader had been killed by a High Priest who led a region called Dovenghar. With the High Flora having conquered the Vharga, Ren escorted the survivors to southern Grandis while being pursued by the High Priest and his forces. Though Ren was able to help the Vharga escape, she was wounded in the skirmish and eventually came across a snake Anima named Saya. Saya was a hundred-year-old snake Anima who possessed the ability to freely transform between her Anima form and a serpent. Known as the legendary Imugi, Saya trained in a lake at the free city of Vallora, as it was said that training for a thousand years would allow her to ascend to the heavens and become a dragon. During her training, however, Saya began wondering whether she could truly become a dragon while secluded in isolation. After saving Ren's life, Saya abandoned her training and traveled the world with Ren, with the two becoming close friends as they explored all the corners of Grandis. Eventually, however, the High Priest pursuing Ren caught her and engaged in a battle, grievously wounding Saya. Nevertheless, Ren was able to injure the High Priest and his soldiers, with the High Priest succumbing to his injuries.

Since that day, Ren hoped to find a way to cure Saya, infusing her strength into her friend at the cost of growing weaker and weaker. Settling in Vallora, Ren worked for the Traveler's Guild as she sought to buy a special elixir that could heal Saya. As Ren undertook missions to earn money for the elixir, a High Flora Priest named Lotusha - the brother of the High Priest whom Ren had killed - arrived in the town on a routine inspection. At the port, Lotusha briefly recognized Ren in the crowd as his brother's murderer and organized an effort to track her down. As the High Flora closed in, Ren obtained enough money to buy the elixir, which she brought to Saya. To her shock, however, Saya revealed that she had known from the beginning that she was going to soon die, and that not even the elixir would heal her. To repay Ren for her kindness, Saya bestowed what remained of her strength to her, while asking that she travel the world and make memories for them both.

After the two said their farewells, Lotusha arrived to kill Ren. However, Ren - after drinking the elixir - defeated him and his soldiers, leaving him alive and injured as she fled. With the High Flora targeting her, Ren worked with the Traveler's Guild to use a hidden Interdimensional Portal in Vallora to travel to Maple World, where she began working with Maple World's Explorers' Guild to grow stronger and help others. During her visits back to Vallora, Ren discovered a girl named Lenna living in Saya's abandoned home with her dog, as she was a refugee from the High Flora war of conquest. Taking pity on the girl, Ren allowed her to stay, believing that it was what Saya would have wanted. As Ren returned back to her full strength, she was summoned by the Traveler's Guild, where she learned that rumors were circulating that Lotusha had become a monster in his quest for greater power, and that he was looking for Ren to get his revenge. After her experiences helping the Nova, Ren wondered whether by 'monster', Lotusha had become a Specter.
