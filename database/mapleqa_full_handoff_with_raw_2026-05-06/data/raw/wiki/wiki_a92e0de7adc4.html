Arcane Symbol: Esfera
Equipment Type Arcane Symbol
Required Level 200
Required Job Any
Extra Stats Level 1:
Arcane Power: +30
Main Stat: +300
HP (Demon Avenger only): +6,300
STR, DEX, and LUK (Xenon only): +144

Every additional level adds:
Arcane Power: +10
Main Stat: +100
HP (Demon Avenger only): +2,100
STR, DEX, and LUK (Xenon only): +48

Max level stats:
Arcane Power: +220
Main Stat: +2,200
HP (Demon Avenger only): +46,200
STR, DEX, and LUK (Xenon only): +1,056
Scroll Enhancements Available None
Max Star Force Enhancements None

  • [Esfera] Mirrors in Mirrors

Additional Symbols:

  • Arcane Symbol: Esfera Coupon
  • Arcane Symbol Selector Coupon (via selection)
Sold for Cannot be sold
Other Information To level up your Arcane Symbol, fuse additional Arcane Symbol: Esfera until you can level up, and pay a Meso fee. See the table below for the costs.
If you wish to transfer your Arcane Symbol to another character, visit Arcane Catalyst to see the requirements.

Voucher

Untradable

Arcane Symbol: Esfera Coupon
Description Double-click to receive Arcane Symbol: Esfera.
Effects Select one of the following (must have completed the quest [Esfera] Mirrors in Mirrors):
  • Combine a specified amount of owned coupons with your equipped Arcane Symbol: Esfera
  • Receive Arcane Symbol: Esfera x 1 in your equipment inventory for every coupon you exchange
Max per slot 9,999
Rewarded from
  • [Daily Quest] Esfera Research Orders (Rewards Arcane Symbol: Esfera Coupon x 20 per day)
  • Esfera Guardian (Rewards Arcane Symbol: Esfera Coupon x 40 per clear, up to 3 clears per week)
Tradability Untradable
Sold for Cannot be sold
Notes None

Untradable, Time Limited

Arcane Symbol: Esfera x 1 Coupon
Description Double-click to obtain the following item:

- Arcane Symbol : Esfera x1.
Effects Select one of the following (must have completed the quest [Esfera] Mirrors in Mirrors):
  • Combine a specified amount of owned coupons with your equipped Arcane Symbol: Esfera
  • Receive Arcane Symbol: Esfera x 1 in your equipment inventory for every coupon you exchange
Max per slot 9,999
Rewarded from Various Events
Bought from
  • Raumo (Lion Coin x 100; 10 Day Duration; MSEA only)
  • Various Event shops
Tradability Untradable
Sold for Cannot be sold
Notes
  • This item is time-limited.
