From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Maple Achievements are a record of a player's adventures and can be earned by accomplishing tasks in MapleStory. They are ranked by difficulty: Normal, Rare, Epic, Unique and Legendary. The harder an achievement is, the more points earned. Points contribute to a player's Achievement Rank and Achievement Rank Emblem and is shared across all worlds and characters for a particular account.
