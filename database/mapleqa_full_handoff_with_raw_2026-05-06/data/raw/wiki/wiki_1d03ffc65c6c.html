NEXT (or Stargazer in GMS) is an update following Milestone. The update includes new HEXA Mastery Nodes, as well as many user experience and quality of life improvements, such as Genesis Weapon Liberation quest improvements, keyboard shortcut improvements, a new Skill Sequence system, as well as movement convenience improvements. In Non-GlobalMS servers[1], the update also includes a new Legion Champion system, a new Level 290+ boss Baldrix, and a new Level 250 Destiny Weapon tier.

There are regional differences between the rollout of the update. In all servers other than KoreaMS and GlobalMS, the update also includes various content released in the 22nd Anniversary Update in KoreaMS, such as various UI revamps as well as skill balancing for all classes. In GlobalMS, the update includes a new job, Sia Astelle as well as a new Erda Link system. In MapleSEA and JapanMS, the update includes a new VIP System replacing the existing MVP System, which was initially introduced in TaiwanMS in the previous Chaser Update. In ChinaMS and TaiwanMS, the update includes a new boss, Malitia, first introduced in Red Moon Forest. In JapanMS, the update includes Red Moon Forest, which was initially introduced in ChinaMS and TaiwanMS in the previous Chaser Update.

Original (KoreaMS)

1st: New HEXA Mastery Nodes and QoL Improvements

  • 3rd and final part of the Reboot World changes, fully converting them into Regular Worlds as Eos and Helios Worlds respectively
  • New 3rd and 4th HEXA Mastery Nodes for every class
  • Cross World Auction House and Meso Market
    • Shared between all regular worlds except Eos and Helios Worlds
    • Equipment and cash items purchasable in the Auction House from other worlds, subject to a Maple Point fee
  • Genesis Weapon Liberation quest improvements
    • Liberation made possible to be done within 3 months, depending on the player's strength
    • Black Mage Genesis Crux clear no longer required to progress, instead requiring Traces of Darkness by defeating bosses from Normal Lotus to Black Mage
  • Keyboard Shortcut improvements
    • Custom Key Settings feature, allowing players to set their desired default layouts for shortcut keys and quick slots which can then be applied to all characters
    • Preset Import feature
  • Skill Sequence System, where a single button press can activate multiple skill buffs in quick succession
    • Every class has predetermined skills that can be placed in this system
  • Arcane River and Grandis changes
    • Storyline reorganization for multiple Arcane River regions
    • Increased number of Arcane Symbols provided by Arcane River Special Content
    • Daily quest completion conditions simplified and gradually decreases based on the number of areas unlocked
    • Symbol Express Pass Maple Point fee reduced
  • Monster Park changes
    • Monster Park Extreme changed to a weekly content asnd can be completed on up to 2 characters per week, with EXP increased to 5x of original
    • Additional EXP on Sundays, including Sunny Sundays, will no longer be applied to Monster Park Extreme
    • Medal acquisition changed to 30 normal Monster Park clears or 3 Monster Park Extreme clears per day of the week
  • Epic Dungeons can be completed on up to 2 characters per week (one per Epic Dungeon) per Maple ID
  • Boss and area quest skip feature, including Arcane River and Grandis areas, now based on Maple ID
  • Sol Erda conversion system, where it can be converted to 30 Faint Sol Erda Energies transferable between characters, or to a HEXA Booster, which works similar to a VIP Booster
  • Separation of Medal and Title appearance and stats
  • UI reorganizations for Quest, World Map, and Minimap
  • Movement convenience improvements
    • Town Portal, where teleports to major towns are available via the NPC and UI
    • New Navigation UI and Return to Town feature
    • World Map Teleport can be used once every 5 minutes
  • Legion attacker placement mode improvements
    • Filter added to separate Legion attackers based on job branches
    • New auto placement mode
  • Guild Name Change, costing 59,900 Maple Points
  • Hyper Burning MAX and Tera Blink events, offering 1+4 level ups from the existing 1+2 level ups
  • Challenger World Season 1, replacing Burning World
    • Provides 3 Tera Burning character slots as well as world-exclusive missions
    • Up to 2 Karma Legendary Potential Scrolls (Level 200) and 20 Ultimate Legion Growth Potions can be obtained
  • Item Burning
    • Existing characters can now use these items
    • New additional challenge goals
  • Morning Star Banquet event
    • Event coins can be obtained instead of the predetermined rewards
  • Winter Tutorial Mission event

2nd: Legion Champion

  • New Legion Champion system, requiring a Level 260+ character and 8,000+ total Legion Level to unlock
    • Complete solo boss missions on Champion Mode on each character to increase Champion rank, which provides bonus stats
      • Champion boss missions are as follows: Hard Lotus, Hard Verus Hilla, Hard Black Mage, Hard Chosen Seren, and Normal Kalos
      • Champion Mode requires clear time within 20 minutes (45 minutes for Black Mage)
    • 2 characters slots available at start, with up to 6 total character slots to fill
      • Only 3 total character slots available on release
      • Unlocking a new character slot requires all characters in the system to have at least defeated Hard Verus Hilla in Champion Mode
  • Champion Raid: Draco Isle
    • Real-time tag-team monthly content where players can swap between Champion characters for up to 3 minutes per character
    • 30-minute Champion's Renown buffs can be acquired depending on the player's performance
  • Official DirectX 11 support and discontinuation of DirectX 9 support
  • Smart AI Calibration option, offering animation smoothing for skill effects and movements (including NPC movements)
  • Intense Power Crystal sell limit reduced to 90 crystals per world
  • Skill Sequence changes
    • Displayed cooldown now based on the currently applied skill with the longest cooldown
    • Displayed cooldown on the shortcut key now reflects cooldown reduction effects
  • Link Skill changes
    • Removal of daily transfer limit and change fees
    • Transfers, removals, and presets can no longer be used in boss maps
  • Guild Skills changes
    • Cooldown reduced to 5 minutes, while reset fee reduced to 5 million mesos
    • Skills can no longer be used in boss maps
  • Epic Dungeon: High Mountain stage difficulty adjustments
  • Burning Express event
  • New Name Auction event
  • Henesys Yutnori Competition event
  • Find the 8000th Day Records

3rd: Sword of the God King, Baldrix

  • New Boss: Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
    • Legion Champion and Champion's Blessing buffs do not apply on the first month of release
  • New Equipment: Oath of Death (second piece of the Brilliant Boss Set)
  • New boss rewards
    • Baldrixroid, Trace of Eternal Loyalty and Baldrix's Soul Shard from Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
    • Eternal Armor of Oaths Box (Eternal Shoes, Gloves, or Cape) and Oath of Death from Baldrix (Hard)
  • Cross World Boss Content
    • Endgame bosses (Extreme Lotus, Black Mage, Hard Chosen Seren, Easy Kalos and higher) can be challenged via the Dimensional Battleground
    • Certain UI and features, as well as usage of most items restricted in the battlefield
  • Boss reward distribution system
    • New distribution option, where rewards are randomly distributed instead of automatically dropping after boss is defeated
    • Certain rewards excluded from this distribution
  • Storage UI changes
    • New Boss Rewards tab holding up to 152 slots, where rewards obtained via Cross World Boss Content and boss reward distribution system can be claimed
    • Items currently equipped will now display as such, and currently equipped chairs and titles can no longer be stored
  • Link Skill changes
    • Transferable to multiple characters at the same time
    • Improved UI, including removal of Currently Applied Link Skills page and application via Preset settings
    • New feature showing Link Skills that are not yet obtained by the player
    • Improved description, showing the jobs required to train each skill
  • Medal UI changes
    • New menu to change the sorting criteria by most recently acquired, oldest acquired, name ascending, and name descending
    • New feature to search for Medals
    • Medals registered in player's collection can be checked via the Medal Collection tab
    • Medal Quest tab changed to allow players to check in-progress and startable medal quests
  • Emoticon and chat improvements
    • Improved emoticon UI, including default slot increase to 40 slots
    • New basic emoticons and Chat Window Settings UI
  • Auction House changes
    • New notification when players attempt to sell items at a price that is excessively low compared to its assessed value
      • Value measured by enhancement level and transaction information from a certain period of time
      • Feature applies to Root Abyss armor, AbsoLab weapons and armor, and Arcane Umbra weapons and armor
  • New achievements related to Legion Champion and Champion Raid
  • Epic Dungeon prerequisite quest skip feature now based on Maple ID
  • New Maple Hands+ Auction House feature, allowing players to search, buy, and re-register items via a mobile app without logging in to the game
    • Cannot be accessed simultaneously on desktop
  • Event UI changes
    • New event start simplification, allowing players to batch start all events if there is a character that has started the event in the same Maple ID
    • New feature to check the current Sunny Sunday benefits by clicking the Sunny Sunday icon
  • Charge! Apostle's Arena event
  • Strange Tides event
  • Champion Burning Double Up event

4th: Destiny Weapon and Star Force Reorganization

  • New Weapon: Destiny Weapon (Level 250+)
    • An upgrade to the existing Genesis Weapon, with increased stats and options
    • Transcendence consists of multiple growth stages, though only 1st growth stage available on release
    • 1st growth stage requires defeating Grandis bosses solo in Destiny Mode
      • Decisive Battles are as follows: Hard Seren (80% final damage reduction), Chaos Kalos (death count reduced to 3), and Hard Kaling (20% increase in final damage)
      • Existing Star Force, base stats, bonus stats, potential, and bonus potential options from Genesis Weapon upgraded to match the Level 250 item
    • Adversary's Resolve required for Transcendence quest progression, obtained by defeating Hard Seren and higher difficulty Grandis bosses
      • Works in a similar fashion to Traces of Darkness for Genesis Weapon Liberation progress
  • Star Force Enhancement reorganization
    • Maximum Star Force limit increased to 30★
    • Existing 23+★ items have their enhancements adjusted to 25+★ (23★ → 25★, 24★ → 28★, 25★ → 30★), with increased scaling to the corresponding Star Force
    • Failed enhancements no longer decrease Star Force, though success and destruction rates adjusted accordingly to compensate
    • Enhancement meso costs increased between 17★ to 19★, and 21+★
    • Safeguard meso costs increased to triple of base meso cost
    • New Sunny Sunday effect: When enhancing items below 21★, destruction rate decreased by 30%
    • Demon Avenger's Star Force Conversion limited to 410★
    • New Star Force-related achievements (26★ to 30★)
  • 3rd HEXA Stat Node available for Level 270+ characters, requiring 2nd Hexa Stat Node to be Level 20
  • Boss improvements
    • New boss rewards
      • Life Boss Ring Box from Kalos (Chaos and Extreme), Kaling (Hard and Extreme), Limbo (Normal and Hard), and Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
        • Replacement of White Jade Boss Ring Boxes previously dropped by the aforementioned bosses
        • Offers higher chances of obtaining Level 4 Special Skill Rings and includes Grindstone of Life as a possible reward
      • Grindstone of Faith from Limbo (Normal and Hard) and Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
        • Level of Special Skill Rings increased to 6
    • Boss reward changes
      • Grindstone of Life removed from Easy Kalos and Easy Kaling drop tables
      • Removal of rank names in Boss Ring Boxes
    • Pattern improvements and changes for Lotus, Damien, Will, Verus Hilla, Black Mage, Seren, Kalos, Kaling, and Baldrix
    • HP reduction for Hard and Extreme Kaling
    • Application of Legion Champion and Champion's Blessing buffs at Baldrix
  • Grandis World Map reorganization
    • Merging of multiple areas into a single area in the UI
      • Area split into Western (Continental Grandis except Erimos), Central (Savage Terminal), and Eastern Grandis (class starting areas and Heliseum)
      • Verdel remains a separate moon, though accessible via the Eastern Grandis World Map
    • New map additions and change of certain map areas
    • Removal and addition of certain NPCs
  • Cross World Boss Content changes
    • Character Change, Hyper Stats reset, preset change, and Guild Skills reuse features made available to use
    • All skills made available to use in Dimensional Battlefield map
  • Mesos limit increased to 2 trillion mesos
  • New Tallahart quests
  • Blessing of the Guild can no longer be used when Greater Blessing of the Guild is active
  • XAudio2 sound system application
  • Champion Challenge event

MapleSEA and JapanMS

Note: This section uses mostly MapleSEA translations as the other Non-KoreaMS servers, except GlobalMS, use similar translated names in their respective languages. Unless otherwise stated, the release order in this section is also mainly based on MapleSEA timeline.

1st: QoL Improvements

Various quality of life improvements introduced in the first three parts in the KoreaMS NEXT Update are rolled out in the first part of the update. In addition, the update also includes:

  • Cross World Auction House, Meso Market, and Boss Content
  • Skill balancing for Kanna
  • Glowing Soul Crystal sell limit reduced to 90 crystals per world
  • Maple Coin expiry reduced to 7 days[2]
  • Hyper Burning MAX and Tera Blink events
  • Challengers World Season 1
  • Double Mush Pass event, similar to Item Burning but with permanent equipment
  • Carling's Special Training event[3]
    • Provides various Advanced EXP Coupons, Sol Erda / Fragment Selector Coupons, as well as Authentic Symbols and Selector Coupons to accelerate leveling from Level 260 to Level 275
    • Rewards also include High Mountain Quick Pass Ticket, Epic Dungeon Bonus Tickets, Transcendent Growth Potion, as well as Carling's Special Potion, providing a level up for characters between Levels 270 and 275
  • Dawnstar Party event
  • Broomy's Special Party event, an overseas exclusive event themed to Dawnstar Party
    • Features Rebel's Punch as well as Maple One Card and Psychic Game of Yut minigames
  • Summer Tutorial Missions event, similar to the Winter Tutorial Missions event introduced in KoreaMS with changes to account for server differences

In JapanMS, the update also includes:

  • Removal of Eastern China and Beasts of Fury[4][5]

2nd: New Mastery Cores and Union Champion

  • New 3rd and 4th Mastery Cores for every class[6]
  • New Union Champion system, requiring a Level 260+ character and 8,000+ total Union Level to unlock
  • Champion Raid: Dragon Island
  • Champion Burning Double Up event

Zipangu and Boss Changes (MapleSEA only)

In MapleSEA, the update also includes:

  • Princess Nou boss revamp
    • Prequest completion no longer required
    • Removal of all previous stages, with minibosses changed to be summoned by Princess Nou for a set duration
    • Increase in difficulty with higher HP, with corresponding increase in Glowing Soul Crystal value
  • Akechi Mitsuhide boss difficulty increase with higher HP per phase, with corresponding increase in Glowing Soul Crystal value
  • Mushroom Shrine map revamp
  • Additional boss missions for Challengers World to include Princess Nou and Akechi Mitsuhide
  • Abyssal Expedition Season 4 - El Nath event[7]
  • MapleStorySEA x NDP60 exclusive collaboration event, in conjunction with Singapore's 60th National Day celebrations

Blood Moon Forest (JapanMS only)

In JapanMS, the update also includes:

  • Blood Moon Forest, a roguelike party content in Jianghu[8]
  • Silent Crusade, replacing the previously exclusive Cross Brigade content
    • Removal of Cross, the boss from the Cross Brigade content, with items dropped being sold at Monster Park shop instead
  • Gollux boss revamp[9]
    • Prerequisite quest simplification, including addition of boss cutscene skip function
    • Higher HP with changes to monster drops and items sold in shops
  • Burning Express event

3rd: UI Revamps, Skill Balancing, and Star Force Reorganization

Various quality of life improvements introduced in the 4th part of the KoreaMS NEXT and all of 22nd Anniversary Updates are rolled out in this part of the update. In addition, the update also includes:

  • Major UI revamps including Login, Main Menu, Equipment, Inventory, Alerts, and Cash Shop
    • Hammer System remains available and renamed to Enhancement Count Increase, though Golden Hammers removed with all available Scroll usages increased by 1[10]
  • Skill balancing for all jobs including Kanna and Hayato, changing 3-minute skill cycles to 2-minute skill cycles
  • 3rd HEXA Stat Core available for Level 270+ characters, requiring 2nd Hexa Stat Core to be Level 20
  • Star Force Enhancement reorganization, similar to the changes introduced in KoreaMS with changes to account for server differences
    • Existing 23+★ items have their enhancements adjusted by 2★ (23★ → 25★, 24★ → 26★, 25★ → 27★), with increased scaling to the corresponding Star Force
    • New Sunday Maple effects
      • When enhancing items below 21★, destruction rate decreased by 30% (40% during certain Special Sunday Maple events)
      • When enhancing items between 22★ and 24★, destruction rate decreased by 20%
  • Boss reward changes
    • Glowing Soul Crystal price adjustments
    • Boss of Life Ring Box from Kalos (Chaos and Extreme), Carling (Hard and Extreme), and Limbo (Normal and Hard)[11]
      • Replacement of White Jade Boss Ring Boxes previously dropped by the aforementioned bosses
  • New VIP System, replacing the existing MVP System[12]
    • New ranks with additional benefits, including exclusive lounge rooms with similar features to the Guild Castle rooms
    • Includes additional quality of life perks and Quick Passes introduced in the MVP System reorganization in KoreaMS
  • Night Troupe in Mu Lung event[13]

Dimensional Tower (MapleSEA only)

In MapleSEA, the update also includes:

  • Dimensional Tower Season 1 event, including ranking system and rewards introduced in Season 2 in KoreaMS
  • Hari Merdeka Malaysia exclusive event, in conjunction with Malaysia's 68th National Day celebrations

Victoria Cup (JapanMS only)

In JapanMS, the update also includes:

  • Victoria Cup (22nd Anniversary) event, including Lord Balrog's World Challenge event

4th: Baldrix and Destiny Weapon

  • New Boss: Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
  • New Equipment: Oath of Death (second piece of the Boss of Brilliant Set)
  • New Weapon: Destiny Weapon (Level 250+)
  • New boss rewards
    • Baldrixroid, Trace of Eternal Fealty and Baldrix's Soul Shard from Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
    • Eternal Oath Armor Box (Eternal Shoes, Gloves, or Cape) and Oath of Death from Baldrix (Hard)
    • Refined Ore of Faith from Limbo (Normal and Hard) and Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
  • Clash of Apostle Battlefield event
  • Champion Challenge event

ChinaMS and TaiwanMS

Note: This section uses mostly MapleSEA translations as the other Non-KoreaMS servers, except GlobalMS, use similar translated names in their respective languages. Unless otherwise stated, the release order in this section is mainly based on TaiwanMS timeline.

1st: New Mastery Cores & QoL Improvements

Various quality of life improvements introduced in the first three parts in the KoreaMS NEXT Update are rolled out in the first part of the update. In addition, the update also includes:

  • New 3rd and 4th Mastery Cores for every class
  • Cross World Auction House and Boss Content
  • Skill balancing for Kanna, Lynn, and Mo Xuan
  • Glowing Soul Crystal sell limit reduced to 90 crystals per world
  • Hyper Burning MAX and Tera Blink events
  • Challengers World Season 1
  • Double Mush Pass event, similar to Item Burning but with permanent equipment
  • Carling's Special Training event
  • Dawnstar Party event
  • Broomy's Special Party event
  • Summer Tutorial Missions event, similar to the Winter Tutorial Missions event introduced in KoreaMS with changes to account for server differences

In ChinaMS, the update also includes:

  • Limit Energy Stone damage cap increase
  • Minimum Combat Power Entry Limit System, replacing minimum damage dealt requirement for boss rewards[14]
    • All party members require meeting a minimum Combat Power to enter specific bosses
  • Additional Challengers World changes
    • All players in Challengers World capped at 700 billion damage
    • Limit Energy Stones obtained from Challengers World cannot be used until the character leaps to regular worlds
    • One of the Challengers Worlds will be maintained and be kept even after the end of the event
      • Players can choose to either leap or keep their characters before the end of event

In TaiwanMS, the update also includes:

  • Cross World Meso Market[15]
  • Maple Coin expiry reduced to 7 days[2][15]
  • Zero class made permanently available[16]
  • MAPLE Daily Gift reorganization[17]
    • Added ability to make up for missed attendances via Maple Points
    • New option to select coins instead of the predetermined rewards
    • New coin shop offering various enhancement items and scrolls for purchase

2nd: Malitia and Union Champion

  • New Boss: Malitia (Normal and Extreme), first introduced in Red Moon Forest
  • New Union Champion system, requiring a Level 260+ character and 8,000+ total Union Level to unlock
  • Star Force Enhancement reorganization, similar to the changes introduced in KoreaMS with changes to account for server differences
    • In ChinaMS, the following changes apply:[18]
      • Existing 20+★ items, except Genesis Weapons or items with Eternal Star Force Scrolls applied, have their enhancements increased by 2★, with increased scaling to the corresponding Star Force
      • Enhancement cost adjustments, with significant price inflation for various enhancement levels compared to other servers
        • 0★ - 14★: Requires Mesos (except Superior items, which still requires Star Tokens)
        • 15★ - 24★: Requires Star Tokens
        • 25★ - 29★: Requires Quenching Star Tokens, usable until the end of current year, and obtained from Extreme Kalos, Extreme Carling, Hard Limbo, Hard Baldrix, and Extreme Malitia
    • In TaiwanMS, the following changes apply:
      • Existing 23+★ items have their enhancements adjusted by 2★ (23★ → 25★, 24★ → 26★, 25★ → 27★), with increased scaling to the corresponding Star Force
      • Success rate adjustments at certain Star Force Enhancements
      • Removal of ability to use Maple Points for enhancements, as previously Maple Points can be used to safeguard enhancements up to 25★
  • Champion Raid: Dragon Island
  • Champion Burning Double Up event

In TaiwanMS, the update also includes:

  • New World: Seren

Stargazer (GlobalMS)

1st: Stargazer

In GlobalMS, the major update is instead named as Stargazer. A new job previously released for MapleStory M, Sia Astelle, the Star Guardian, is added in this update.

Various quality of life improvements introduced in the first three parts in the KoreaMS NEXT Update are rolled out in the first part of the update. In addition, the update also includes:

  • New Area: Oort, the starting area for Sia Astelle
  • New Erda Link system, a HEXA Matrix replacement exclusive to Sia Astelle
  • Cross World Auction House and Meso Market
    • Maple Point fee for Cross World Auction House waived
  • Skill balancing for Kanna and Lynn
  • Auto-Harvest System improvements, including hotkey, notification feature, and increased coin limit with reduced daily Auto-Harvest limit
  • DirectX 11 support
  • Hyper Burning MAX and Tera Blink events
  • Challenger World Season 1, replacing Burning World
    • Characters able to be transferred to either Interactive or Heroic Worlds
    • Interactive and Heroic Worlds have separate Challenger Worlds
  • Item Burning
    • Existing characters can now use these items
    • New additional challenge goals
  • Wish Upon a Shooting Star event (Sia Astelle's growth event), including a Premium Pass and a Fashion Pass
  • Hyperion World Leap event, allowing players to leap their characters from Kronos to Hyperion but not vice versa
    • Players who participate in Challenger World and leap to Hyperion get additional Ultimate Legion Growth Potions
  • Morning Star Banquet event
  • Summer Tutorial Mission event, similar to the Winter Tutorial Mission event introduced in KoreaMS with changes to account for server differences

The following changes from KoreaMS are not implemented:

  • Reboot World changes
  • Cross World Boss Content
  • Intense Power Crystal sell limit reduction

2nd: MapleStory x Dave the Diver

  • New 3rd and 4th HEXA Mastery Nodes for every class
  • Skill balancing for Kanna, Hayato, Mo Xuan and Lynn
  • HEXA Booster changed to SHINE Booster in Erda Link UI
  • Challenger World Hunting Mission - 2x Points Event, offering doubled points for completing Hunting Missions until the end of the event
  • Dave the Diver collaboration event[19]
  • Ride or Die Season 2 exclusive event
    • Rewards include a new Shackles of Resentment, a badge with temporary stats and potential, as well as Chains of Resentment (Interactive Worlds only), and an additional Event Legion Block
  • Burning Express event
  • Tinkerer's Chest event (Interactive and Challenger - Interactive Worlds only)
  • Extended Ursus Golden Time event (EU Challenger World only)

    • MSEA/TMS/JMS: NEXT
    • CMS: 下一站 (Next Stop; Xiàyízhàn)
  • In KoreaMS, this is the update that includes the last part of the conversion of Reboot Worlds into Regular Worlds. All updates from this point will no longer have any Reboot Worlds.
  • In KoreaMS, the theme songs of this update are NEXT ONE and NEXT TO, and the singer is Ha Hyun-woo (KR: 하현우).
    • The instrumental version of NEXT ONE was temporarily used as the Login BGM in the first part of the update.
  • In KoreaMS, to further alleviate increasing population pressure in Continental Grandis maps, the maximum number of players that can share an instanced map is temporarily increased to 4 players.
    • This was initially introduced during the second part of the New Age Update with a cap of 2 players sharing each map, and has remained as such until in between of the third and fourth parts of this update.[20]
    • This change was also implemented in MapleSEA in the third part of the update, and in GlobalMS in the Carcion Octo Fest Update.
  • In GlobalMS, the Login BGM was temporarily changed to the Stargazer theme in this update.
  • A number of regional differences came across with this update:
    • In Non-KoreaMS servers, the following differences apply:
      • Quality of life improvement changes introduced in the first 3 parts of the update were consolidated and rolled out in the first part of the update.
      • The following features were not implemented in this update:
        • Maple Home system, due to an already existing Home system introduced before this update.
        • Maple Hands+ Auction House feature, due to the app only available for KoreaMS.
    • In some Non-KoreaMS servers, parts of this update were added in the previous Milestone / Chaser Update:
      • In TaiwanMS and JapanMS, the conversion of Reboot Worlds into Regular Worlds was completed during the third part of the Chaser Update.
      • In ChinaMS and TaiwanMS, Hyper Burning MAX event was rolled out in the first part of the Chaser Update.
    • In MapleSEA and GlobalMS, the 3rd and 4th mastery nodes were delayed to the second part of the update. In JapanMS, these nodes were released a few weeks after the first part of the update.
    • In GlobalMS, Star Force Reorganization was delayed to the Every Little Thing, Every Precious Thing Update due to changes in obtaining and restoring equipment introduced during that update.
