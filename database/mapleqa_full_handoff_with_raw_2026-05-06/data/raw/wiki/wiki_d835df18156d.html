Intense Power Crystal is an item dropped by most Bosses and can be sold to the Collector for a certain amount of Mesos based on the boss.

Intense Power Crystals have a base price, and these prices are adjusted in major updates.

The crystals are split into three types: Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Crystals.

Intense Power Crystal
Description A soul crystal left behind by someone with intense power. The crystal is divided equally amongst party members. Has no special function, but you can sell it at a high price to a Collector who hangs out at the Free Market Entrance. But remember, the Collector only purchases set quantities during specific time frames.
Max per slot 1
Sold for See below for Meso(s)
Dropped by See below for mob
Tradability Untradable
Notes
  • This item is time-limited.
    • Duration: 7 days
  • This item can only be sold to Collector.
    • Limit of 180 per world per week, 14 Weekly per character per week (GMS).
    • Limit of 90 per world per week, 12 Weekly per character per week (Non-GMS).
    • Difference of Mesos will be provided when higher value Weekly Crystals are sold.
