Hyper Stats

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Hyper Stats are a system of obtaining additional stats, starting at Level 140, with points obtained for each level obtained beyond 140. The points can be used to increase various stats, such as main stats, critical rate, and damage.

Hyper Stats have presets, which allow the player to swap between three sets of allocated points for 2,000,000 Mesos. In GMS, there is no cost to change between presets. Hyper Stats can also be reset for 10,000,000 Mesos.
