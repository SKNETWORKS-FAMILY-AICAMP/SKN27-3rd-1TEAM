Mirror World is a Manipulated World that exists alongside Maple World within the same dimension. As the limitation of the Transcendent of Time is that they cannot change the future which they have foreseen, the Goddess Rhinne realized that she could not stop the Black Mage from stealing her power. For this reason, she created the Child of the Goddess so that they would succeed her as the new Transcendent of Time in the distant future, thereby ensuring that the Transcendent of Time's power would not remain in the Black Mage's hands. To this end, Rhinne created a world infused with the power of time, intending to mask her child's aura from the Black Mage. However, a scholar named Will who recently became a Commander of the Black Mage, used Rhinne's power to locate the Child of the Goddess and remake her world into an imperfect replica of Maple World called Mirror World, after which he split the Child of the Goddess into two beings, imprisoning one in their own temple and brainwashing the other to serve in his Shadow Knights. Zero characters start here upon creation, with their questline from Level 100 - 180 centered around escaping Mirror World. Throughout the storyline, both Zero Alpha and Beta form close ties with the residents of Mirror World.

This region can only be accessed by Zero characters by using the Temple Recall skill to teleport back to Zero's Temple. Zero characters slowly gain access to each town / region as they progress through their introductory questline.

Towns / Regions

Zero's Temple

Main article: Zero's Temple

Zero's Temple is a temple created by Rhinne for the Child of the Goddess. It is home to the priests who were hand-picked by Rhinne to protect her successor, and it serves as the home base for Alpha and Beta as they collect the Goddess Teardrops throughout their storyline.

Shadowvale

Main article: Shadowvale

Shadowvale is a town created by Will to serve as the headquarters of the Shadow Knights. Unlike the other regions of Mirror World, Shadowvale is the only known region that does not have a corresponding counterpart on Maple World. Zero characters start here upon creation, with a portion of their introductory questline centered around learning the truth about the Child of the Goddess.

Mirror World Leafre

Main article: Mirror World Leafre

Mirror World Leafre is a town modeled after Leafre in Maple World. This town is home to the Halflingers and dragons, including Afrien and Mir, as well as a replica of Freud, who studies the dragons of the region. This area is where Chapter 1 of Zero's storyline takes place, focusing on Alpha and Beta recovering the Transcendent of Time's relic.

Mirror World Ariant

Main article: Mirror World Ariant

Mirror World Ariant is a town modeled after Ariant in Maple World. This desert region is home to the greedy Queen Areda, as well as the Sand Bandits who hope to take back the town for the people of Ariant. This area is where Chapter 2 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must recover the first Goddess Teardrop to regain their Transcendent powers. Upon collecting the Goddess Teardrop at the end of the Mirror World Ariant storyline, Zero characters can leave Mirror World and gain EXP in Maple World.

Mirror World Henesys

Main article: Mirror World Henesys

Mirror World Henesys is a town modeled after Henesys in Maple World, with two special dungeons modeled after Gold Beach and Sleepywood respectively. This area is where Chapter 3 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must find the second Goddess Teardrop.

Mirror World Mu Lung

Main article: Mirror World Mu Lung

Mirror World Mu Lung is a town modeled after Mu Lung in Maple World. This area is home to the powerful warriors of the Mu Lung Dojo, as well as painters and artisans who hone their craftsmanship. This area is where Chapter 4 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must find the third Goddess Teardrop.

Mirror World Edelstein

Main article: Mirror World Edelstein

Mirror World Edelstein is a town modeled after Edelstein in Maple World. Similar to its Maple World counterpart, Mirror World Edelstein has been invaded by the Black Wings, with the Resistance working to liberate their city. This area is where Chapter 5 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta are thwarted by Will, who dispatches a false Goddess Teardrop to trick them.

Mirror World Magatia

Main article: Mirror World Magatia

Mirror World Magatia is a town modeled after Magatia in Maple World, home to the feuding Zenumist and Alcadno societies. This area is where Chapter 6 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must face Rachael, a false Goddess Teardrop created by Will to impede their progress.

Mirror World Ludibrium

Main article: Mirror World Ludibrium

Mirror World Ludibrium is a town modeled after Ludibrium in Maple World, where the flow of time has been disrupted by the broken clocktowers that support the town. This area is where Chapter 7 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must recover the final two Goddess Teardrops, as well as to repair the temporal collapse threatening the balance of Mirror World Ludibrium.

Mirror World Temple of Time

Main article: Mirror World Temple of Time

Mirror World Temple of Time is a town modeled after the Temple of Time in Maple World, home to replicas of the Guardians of Time who serve the Goddess Rhinne. This area is where Chapter 8 of Zero's storyline takes place, in which Alpha and Beta must receive the counsel of Rhinne's priests in order to determine which of the two has been corrupted by Will.

Mirror World Kritias

Main article: Mirror World Kritias

Mirror World Kritias is a town modeled after Kritias in Maple World. Unlike the other regions of Mirror World, Mirror World Kritias is not part of Zero's class storyline, and does not have a town questline or NPCs (aside from Oko), instead only having a special dungeon that requires a Spring of Rebirth, purchasable from Cello in Zero's Temple. Completing this dungeon awards EXP, Weapon Points for Zero's weapon, and  Time Coins.

Story

Centuries after both Rhinne and the Black Mage were sealed away, a scholar named Will, who had grown obsessed with the Transcendents and their motives, deduced the existence of Rhinne's child and the world in which she had hidden them. After the Black Mage's resurrection, Will became a loyal Commander and used the power of an imprisoned Rhinne to locate the Child of the Goddess. Tasked by the Black Mage with keeping Rhinne's child imprisoned, Will - inspired by the power of the devil Ergoth Dunamis, who had once created a replica of the kingdom of Sharenian - used Rhinne's power to remake her world into an imperfect replica of Maple World called Mirror World, after which he split the Child of the Goddess into two beings, imprisoning one in their own temple and brainwashing the other to serve in his Shadow Knights.

After the brainwashed child, Alpha, discovered the truth and freed his counterpart, Beta, the two Children of the Goddess - aided by the priests tasked by Rhinne with protecting her child - began a journey to recover the Goddess Teardrops, containing the power of time, in order to awaken as the new Transcendents of Time and escape Mirror World. After defeating Will and freeing Rhinne, Alpha and Beta were bestowed the full power of the Transcendents by Rhinne, causing Mirror World - which was being maintained with her power - to disappear. However, Alpha and Beta, having formed close bonds with the people of Mirror World, used that power to recreate Mirror World immediately upon its destruction, allowing for Mirror World's existence to be maintained at the cost of the twins losing the power which Rhinne had given them, thereby making them imperfect, incomplete Transcendents.
