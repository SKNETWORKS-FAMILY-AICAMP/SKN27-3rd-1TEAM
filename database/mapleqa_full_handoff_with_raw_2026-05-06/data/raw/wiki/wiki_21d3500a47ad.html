Traits

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Personality Traits (or simply Traits) are a system that allow characters to gain and improve upon various abilities. A character's traits appear in the Professions UI when the character is above Level 30. Traits of other characters can viewed in the character window when clicking on them.

There are six types of Traits - Ambition, Insight, Empathy, Willpower, Diligence and Charm. Traits can be leveled up from 1 to 100. There is a daily limit of 500 EXP per day for each trait except Charm, which has a daily limit of 5,000. Characters can obtain special medals upon reaching Level 20, 60, and 100 after completing a quest for each trait.
