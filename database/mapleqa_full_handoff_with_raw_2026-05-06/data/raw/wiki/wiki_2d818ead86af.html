A chair is an item that player characters can sit on to regain Health and Mana. Chairs are commonly used while chatting, waiting or idling.

Chairs can be obtained through quests, bosses, events or Gachapon, chairs that are no longer obtainable may return to gachapon or rewarded from external media such as Twitch drops/giveaways.

Cube Chairs can be stacked together up to 3 stacks, with 6 cubes in each stack, and your character will sit on top of the middle stack.
