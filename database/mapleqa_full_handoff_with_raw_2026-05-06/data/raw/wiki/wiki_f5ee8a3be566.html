NEO is an update following Awake. This update introduces EXP reductions, Level/Damage/Meso cap increment, as well as New Nova Archer Class, Kain. It also focuses the main story plot towards the post-Black Mage era, where both Maple World and Grandis has merged with the introduction of Western Grandis (formerly Continental Grandis) regions such as Cernium and Hotel Arcus.

1st: Signal

  • Neo Growth Revamp
    • Level cap increased from 275 to 300
    • EXP reduction by 20% from Level 210 to 250
    • Damage cap increased to 150 billion
    • Meso cap increased to 100 billion
    • Monster Park: Lachelein, the Dreaming City
    • Arcane Symbol rewards increased (Vanishing Journey, Chu Chu Island)
    • Tenebris bosses (Gloom, Verus Hilla, Darknell and Black Mage) will have a chance to drop Arcane Umbra equipment
  • Quality of Life Updates
  • Grandis map updated to include Continental Grandis along with two sub-areas.
    • New Area: Cernium (Level 260+)
    • New Area: Burning Cernium (Level 265+)
  • New System: Authentic Force / Sacred Power for Continental Grandis maps, similar to Arcane Force
  • Neo Castle Event

2nd: Chaser of Darkness, Kain

  • New Nova Archer, Kain
  • New Area: Toolen City, beside the polluted seas of Savage Terminal

3rd: Hotel Arcus

  • New Boss: Chosen Seren
  • New Area: Hotel Arcus (Level 270+)

Trivia

  • Server Naming Difference:
    • GMS: Neo
    • KMS: NEO (네오, Neo)
    • JMS: NEO
    • MSEA: NEO
    • TMS: NEO
    • CMS: 新纪元 (New era, Xīn jìyuán)
  • A 64-bit client was released for public testing in KoreaMS Tespia before the first NEO Update. According to the Tespia notes, this was scheduled for release on September 2021.
  • In KoreaMS, the theme song of this update is NEO, and the singer is Ha Hyun-woo (KR: 하현우).
  • In ChinaMS, the Star Force Enhancement System changes were introduced. This was introduced in Adventure update in all other regions.
  • All servers have Level 300 players.
    • In GlobalMS, Niru from Aurora World reached Level 300 on May 23, 2024.[1]
    • In TaiwanMS, 猀儿 from Alicia World reached Level 300 on December 13, 2024.[2]
    • In KoreaMS, 오지환 from Luna World reached Level 300 on January 27, 2025.[3]
    • In ChinaMS, 丘啊丘 from Lucid World reached Level 300 on May 25, 2025.[4][5]
    • In MapleSEA, lqsKniGhT from Cassiopeia World reached Level 300 on January 17, 2026.[6]
    • In JapanMS, うづき from Kaede World reached Level 300 on February 14, 2026.[7][8]
  • The initials of the words Glory, Rise, Awake, NEO, Destiny, Ignition and Savior form Grandis.
