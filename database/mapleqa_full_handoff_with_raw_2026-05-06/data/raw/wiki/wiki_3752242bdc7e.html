Equipment

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Equipment are items designed to augment a character's abilities or to change cosmetic looks. It has several different categories made to fit different parts of the character. In order to access higher-level equipment, players need to level up and meet the minimum required statistics (stats) requirements.

Most equipment in the game are class specific, but there are also a fair amount of equipment that can be worn by any class as long as the character meets the minimum level and stats.

Equipment Set

Set Items are equipment that grant various bonus effects if the player equips a specified number of equipment of the same set, and bonuses from Set Item stats are separately applied on top of the equipped equipment's stats.[1]
