Beyond (or Override in GMS) is an update following V which introduced new and revamped maps, a new system and new 5th job skills for all jobs.

Prelude: WONDER WINTER

  • New Arcane River Special Content in Lachelein: Dream Defender
  • Mu Lung Dojo update, expanding the tower to the 63rd Floor
  • Minar Forest Map Changes
  • Magatia Questline Reorganization

1st: EVOLVE

  • New 5th Job skills for all jobs
  • New Boss Difficulty: Lucid [Hard Mode]
  • Theme Dungeon Updates: Lion King's Castle, Crimsonheart Castle, and Golden Temple

2nd: UNION

  • New System: Legion System
    • This combines the Character Cards & Part-Time Jobs systems
  • New Theme Dungeon: Omega Sector
    • Omega Sector reopens after it was closed since the You & I Update
  • New Star Force Maps added in Lion King's Castle, Kerning Tower, and Omega Sector
  • Various quality of life updates and system improvements

3rd: ARCANA

  • Arcane River's 4th Area: The Mystical Forest of Arcana
  • New Boss Difficulty: Lucid [Story Mode]
  • Theme Dungeon Update: Fantasy Theme World
