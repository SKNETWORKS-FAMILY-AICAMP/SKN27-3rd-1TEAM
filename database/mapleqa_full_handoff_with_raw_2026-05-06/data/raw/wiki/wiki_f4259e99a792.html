A mysterious island floating in the sky, and the home base of Empress Cygnus and her Knights. Long protected by a sacred aura, this island is where the Cygnus Knights gather, swearing their vows to safeguard the peace of Maple World. An air of elegance and nobility breezes over the entire island. Under the Empress's rule, the Knights devote themselves to training for the sake of peace.
It's said that all Knights born on this island are destined to fight against the forces of darkness.

“A floating island that watches over the entirety of Maple World. Under the protection of Shinsoo, Maple World's empress, Cygnus, resides here with her ever-faithful knights.”

― In-game description for Ereve

Ereve is a floating island near Victoria Island and the starting area for Cygnus Knights. Ereve is the capital of Maple World and the seat of power of the Empress. Long ago, the Divine Bird Shinsoo created the island after being tasked with watching over the Empress' bloodline. For ages, Shinsoo and her descendants, the bird-like Piyo race, protected the Empress alongside the Noblesse - a group of humans who had sworn to uphold Shinsoo's ideals to protect others. Centuries ago, the Black Mage and his Commanders attacked Ereve and killed Empress Aria, leaving her descendants to scatter and hide in obscurity, and the Noblesse to go extinct over the next several centuries. Hundreds of years later, Aria's descendant - Empress Cygnus - inherited the throne and formed the Cygnus Knights to fight against the Black Mage and his minions, opening the gates to those who wish to fight under her banner. Cygnus Knight characters start here upon creation, with their introductory questline centered around Ereve.

This region can be accessed via airship by talking to Kiriru at the Station to Ereve in the Six Path Crossway. The island of Ereve itself is part of the Arteria map, which spans both Ereve and the battleship Arteria, although this region is classified as part of Western Grandis following the convergence of Maple World and Grandis, and is only accessible at level 280.
