Anima

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory

The Anima are one of the three major races of Grandis, alongside the Flora and the Nova. There are currently three playable classes in the Anima race: Hoyoung, Lara and Ren.

History

Era of the Ancient Gods

During the era of the Ancient Gods, the Anima lived in insular tribes in various regions of Grandis. One such group of Anima were the snake and turtle Anima of Carcion, who lived under the protection of the Ancient God Keira. As the chaos of the world grew, twelve of the most outstanding Anima sages were chosen by the Overseers, who had taken notice of the sages and their strong sense of duty and responsibility to help the world. These twelve Anima became known as the elders of Grandis, who were then tasked by the Overseers to conduct experiments to create beings that could defeat the Ancient Gods and quell the chaos of the world. These elders, along with many Anima researchers, began experiments in Odium to create Adversaries by fusing them with the Godspheres. To protect Odium from those who were threatened by or coveted the power of the Adversary, the elders called in the four strongest Anima in the world - Ara the Azure Dragon, Shuli the Vermillion Bird, Baekyeon the White Tiger, and Gaon the Black Tortoise - who became known as the Four Symbols. Over the course of many years, the elders created Adversaries through brutal experiments that stripped away their humanity, leaving them empty shells obsessed only with fulfilling their assigned destiny. This lack of humanity was known as contamination, which slowly began infecting the elders and the researchers.

Kaling, a researcher with a kind heart, underwent the painful experiments, though she was deemed a defective Vessel. However, the contamination began infecting her, leaving her obsessed with fulfilling her responsibility as an Adversary. Because of this, she created four machines that would consume monsters across Grandis in order to gather power, after which she would fuse them with a Vessel to create an extremely powerful Adversary. However, the contamination eventually stripped her of her humanity and morality, and her monsters - the Four Perils - began terrorizing Grandis. Kaling and her monsters were later sealed away by Tai Yu, another defective Vessel, on the elders' orders. After the end of the war, the elders realized that the contamination was spreading critically into them, and so they decided to sequester themselves away from the world. Weio, the sheep Anima elder, created the bell mysticant alongside several other elders, which they believed could undo their contamination and wash away the sins that they had committed. Weio left the bell with a sheep Anima village, entrusting them with finding someone who had a pure enough heart to make the bell ring and absolve them of their sins. The researchers then created the Constables, robotic dolls made in the image of the researchers from before the contamination had touched them. With the Constables maintaining Odium and guarding their research, the elders, the researchers, and the Four Symbols created Shangri-La, the realm of the sages, where they imprisoned themselves in order to stop the contamination from both worsening inside them, as well as to stop it from spreading to the outside world.

Disappearance

During the Flora Civil War, the High Flora conducted special rituals on the other races of Grandis in order to transform them into Specters for their army. Many Anima fell victim to the ethnic cleansing campaign of the High Flora, with entire villages being eradicated during the war. By the time that Aboris fell, the Anima were believed to have all but disappeared. Unknown to many, however, the Anima secluded themselves in the most remote parts of Grandis. Many Anima even began the practice of hiding their animal features in order to prevent themselves from being targeted, similar to members of the Black Nova cult removing their Nova features. There is at least one known instance of an Anima even living in Maple World - Rondo - though it remains unknown when or how he crossed dimensions, or whether he even is indeed an Anima. During her travels, a wandering poet named Willow encountered a gladiator whom he believed to be an Anima hiding his features.

The Sage's Apprentice

During the wars of Grandis, Tai Yu - who had become a legendary sage - discovered that the white tiger Anima village had been attacked, resulting in their extinction, save for an infant child that Tai Yu rescued. He took the child back to his Hermitage in Shangri-La and named him Hoyoung. Many years later, Hoyoung accidentally freed Taotie, one of the Four Perils of Grandis, and unleashed the many fiends that Taotie had consumed over the ages. Hoyoung and Taotie then went on a mission to catch the released fiends and became unlikely friends over time. Hoyoung later joined the Maple Alliance during his quest to catch the fiends and faced Kaling, who had lost her humanity to the contamination and had become an Apostle of Darmoor.

Innocent Earthseer

In the secluded Anima village that had been entrusted with the bell mysticant, a young sheep Anima named Lara was able to make the bell glow in her hands. With her strong affinity for the elemental spirits, Lara went on a mission to make the bell ring and became friends with a wolf Anima named Gri on Narin. Together, they were able to face a woman named Hannah, who possessed an evil paintbrush mysticant. Lara was able to make the bell ring, which erased Hannah and the paintbrush, after which Lara went on a journey to learn more about the bell. During her travels, she faced Kaling, who was attempting to collect the mysticants. Lara also joined the Maple Alliance in order to help the people of Maple World while on her quest.

Red-Eyed Wanderer

A rabbit Anima named Ren worked as a mercenary who helped the people of Grandis escape High Flora persecution. During her travels, she encountered a legendary snake Anima named Saya, who had been training for decades to become a dragon. After Saya saved Ren's life, she abandoned her training and traveled with Ren across the world. After Saya was severely wounded at the hands of a High Flora officer, Ren began working for the Traveler's Guild in Vallora to purchase medicine for her friend. However, another High Flora officer named Lotusha tracked Ren down to avenge his brother - the officer whom Ren had killed. After Saya passed from her injuries, Ren defeated Rotusha and left Grandis to train in Maple World, joining the Maple Alliance in order to fulfill Saya's last wish to protect others.

Vulpes

On Vulpes, the Pointy-Ear Fox Anima lived in seclusion, protected by Sino the Fox God. Under her protection, the fox Anima were given guardian spirits, with which they could hunt and protect themselves. The forgotten hero, Shade, awoke on Vulpes centuries after his battle against the Black Mage and was given the power of the spirits by the Fox God. Later on, an unknown Apostle of Gerand Darmoor attempted to flood the fox Anima, though their plan was foiled by the combined efforts of a traveler, a fox Anima named Maru, and the Fox God herself.

Angler Company

During his experiments to become an Adversary, the Angler Father kidnapped a rabbit Anima and used his heart to create an imitation Godsphere, as well as turning the rabbit Anima into a cyborg, implied to be the Angler Father's secondary body 'Alter', which was used to disguise himself as a Constable of Odium. Later on, the rabbit Anima's friends attempted to save him by infiltrating Angler Company headquarters, though they were all slaughtered by Cosim on the Angler Father's orders. As Cosim's heart was a failed experiment created from the rabbit Anima's heart, Cosim inherited the rabbit Anima's memories and mistakenly believed that he himself was the rabbit Anima, prompting him to betray his father. Cosim would then steal the imitation Godsphere from his father, which was later inherited by his sister, Senya Angler.

Shangri-La

During their journey to restore their broken Seal Stone, the Adversary obtained the World Heart in Odium and traveled to Shangri-La with Tai Yu's help, where they met the elders. Kaling, with the help of several elders who had joined Darmoor, broke into Shangri-La and unleashed the full power of her three Perils in order to take the World Heart. The Adversary defeated Kaling, who was killed when the World Heart drained her powers, after which the elders tasked the Adversary with restoring the World Heart. They also explained that the bell mysticant had failed to cleanse them when Lara had made it ring, though they nevertheless vowed to make amends for the sins that they had committed.

Carcion

While attempting to stop the Apostles from taking the power of the gods, the Alliance traveled to Carcion, where they discovered that the snake and turtle Anima tribes were at war over the divine water blessed by the Ancient God Keira. In order to seize control of Carcion, the High Flora had dispatched Specters capable of disguising themselves as Anima in order to confuse each tribe into thinking that the other had betrayed Keira and taken over her sanctuary. However, with the help of the Alliance, the Anima tribes put aside their differences and worked together to fight the High Flora, who had kidnapped several Anima for experimentation and were attempting to steal Keira's power. In order to defeat the Apostle Limbo, the Anima surrendered Keira's blessings to the goddess, who used their power to deal a decisive blow to Limbo. Following the battle, Keira used the remainder of her power to cleanse Specter pollution in Carcion's waters, after which the Anima were tasked with protecting their home in her absence. With the two tribes ending their feud, the Anima sought to open their borders and unite the various Anima tribes scattered across Grandis.

Society and Culture

Unlike the Flora and the Nova, the Anima are a disparate people with no capital or large community. The Anima are largely pacifistic and have little to no military strength. As a result, many Anima have been subjugated under the rule of the High Flora. The few Anima settlements that exist in Grandis are largely secluded and remote, though the growing High Flora presence in the galaxy means that these remote settlements are being discovered with increased frequency. As a persecuted race, the Anima typically experience fear and discrimination, not only from the High Flora, but also from other minor races, due to being uncommon and misunderstood. For this reason, many Anima who choose not to live in an Anima community typically attempt to hide their Anima features with magic or clothing. In some cases, however, hiding one's features is impossible, such as in the case of Farasi, who cannot hide her blue skin like she hides the rest of her aquatic features. Though most Anima do not have the ability to change into animals, there are instances of certain Anima possessing this power, such as Saya and Sino. While Sino is a Great Spirit of Grandis, Saya is a rare Imugi Anima said to have the ability to become a dragon after training for a thousand years, though Saya abandoned her training to help others with Ren.

The Anima are the most spiritually attuned of the three major races, with a strong affinity for nature and spirits. Many Anima find themselves under the protection of the spirits, such as Lara and the Pointy-Ear Foxes. During the era of the Ancient Gods, the Anima played a major role in quelling the chaos of the world, as the elders and the researchers had been hand-picked by the Overseers for their strong sense of duty and morality. Their spirituality and affinity for natural harmony can been in the creation of Shangri-La - a convergence of the four seasons, whose natural energy can counteract the contamination that strips away one's humanity. The bell mysticant created by the elders also holds strong spiritual power, which responds to an exceptionally pure heart. This purity of heart is also what naturally attracts spirits, as seen with Lara and Gri.

Despite being pacifistic, the Anima have the advantages of their animal features giving them heightened senses and instincts, which can give them an edge in battle, as seen with the mysterious Anima gladiator, as well as with Rondo's greater hearing abilities. Additionally, Anima such as Hoyoung and Lara have considerable magical talent and have demonstrated great feats of power. Despite the Nova being classified as a separate race from the Anima, there have been two known instances of Anima with dragon features - Ara the Azure Dragon and Chenyuan the dragon Anima elder. Ara and Chenyuan possess dragon horns in the Eastern style of real-world dragons, and have no wings or a tail, which appears to be the main difference between Nova and dragon Anima. Though unconfirmed, it is possible that dragon Anima are a result of special training, as Saya was a snake Anima who had been training to become a dragon after a thousand years, though she abandoned this training prematurely. This spiritual training would be consistent with the fact that both Ara and Chenyuan possess immense spiritual power, possibly as a result of ascending to dragonhood.
