“A town with a temple worshipping an ancient Nova god. After the Nova capital was seized, the Nova took refuge in Pantheon and made it their new home. To protect the town, a Sacred Stone was installed on its border in the four cardinal directions to erect a barrier.”

― In-game description for Pantheon

Pantheon is a town build on an island in Grandis's eastern region. It is the sanctuary city of the Nova people where the Nova evacuated to after the fall of the capital city of Heliseum decades ago at the hands of the traitor Magnus. While the Former Kaiser bought time for the evacuation, the Nova priests - unable to transport the Outrigger, the holy relic of the Nova - instead transferred a portion of its power into four sacred stones, which were then brought to Pantheon to create a Protective Shield that prevented the Specter army from entering the city. Decades later, Magnus prepares to attack Pantheon and seize the power of the sacred stones for himself. Kaiser and Angelic Buster characters start here upon creation, with their introductory questline centered around mastering their new abilities, as well as holding off Magnus' invasion of Pantheon.

This region can be accessed on foot by via the Interdimensional Portal at the Six Path Crossway. It is divided into four sections: North Pantheon, South Pantheon, East Pantheon, and West Pantheon.
