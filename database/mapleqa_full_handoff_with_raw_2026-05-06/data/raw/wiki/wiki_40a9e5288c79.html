Spiegelmann's Gonzo Gallery

Closed Content / Feature
NOTE: This content / feature has been closed temporarily and will remain inaccessible until further notice.


Spiegelmann's Gonzo Gallery was a theme dungeon meant for level 50 - 120 players, where Spiegelmann hosted an art gallery. By looking at the paintings themed around the four seasons, you could travel into each of them and complete a series of quests. Fall was themed around Leafre and involved you helping Spiegelmann to hatch what he believes to be a dragon egg (only for a white tiger to appear from the egg). Winter was themed around El Nath and involved you trying to escape the colds and create a makeshift rope out of werewolf tails; you would later find Spiegelmann carving Zakum at the end. Spring was themed around Edelstein and involved you helping Spiegelmann prepare his mustache for the season. Summer was themed around Ariant and involved assisting Spiegelmann attempt to woo Queen Areda.

After completing all 4 of the Seasonal Series, you would be able to travel into Spiegelmann's Unpublished Work where you could fight a mini-boss, the Spiegelframe. Completion of the theme dungeon would allow you to get various accessories.

Spiegelmann's Gonzo Gallery was temporarily closed as of the You & I / Rising Heroes Update, and has yet to return.
