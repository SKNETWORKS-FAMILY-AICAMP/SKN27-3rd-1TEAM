Removed Content / Feature
This article contains information no longer in-game, and are thus historical records of MapleStory.

Arboren was the starting area for Beast Tamer. It was the third area added to a continent called Dawnveil, which also contained Tynerum (consisting of Crimsonheart Castle and Shadow Veil Forest) as well as Commerci Republic. However, the concept of Dawnveil was scrapped, and all the regions within were separated into their own areas.

Arboren was a forest-themed village with creatures of a race different from Beast Tamer herself. The monsters in the area were wolves and Kobolds, with Beast Tamer's starting quests requiring her to rescue the villagers from the Kobolds.

The area, along with Beast Tamer, existed in ChinaMS, JapanMS, TaiwanMS, and GlobalMS. As of 2024, Beast Tamer characters were all converted to Lynn characters and new Beast Tamer characters can no longer be created. Arboren was removed from the game as a result.
