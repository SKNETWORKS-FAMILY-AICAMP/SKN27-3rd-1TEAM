World Archive
“The progenitor of the Black Slimes of Gold Beach. Its host is a giant mutant octopus.
Possesses a greater intelligence than other Black Slimes and is highly driven to create more of its kind.
Its host octopus is also very powerful, but it is unable to dive to its native waters because of the slime on its head. Unable to swim in shallower water, it rides in an inner tube.”
― Chronicler Ael

Captain Darkgoo is the final boss of the Gold Beach theme dungeon. He is depicted as a giant black slime wearing a captain's hat and floating on a blue inflatable tube. As the main cause of the island's chaos, Captain Darkgoo spreads black slimes across the island using his massive submarine. During the battle, he summons Starfish Octopus Slimes and Seashell Octopus Slimes to assist him. Additionally, he attacks by smashing coconuts, which he holds in his arms, onto the ground.
