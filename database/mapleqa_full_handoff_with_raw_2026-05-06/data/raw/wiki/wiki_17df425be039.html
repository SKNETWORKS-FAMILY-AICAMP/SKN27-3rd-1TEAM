Home, also known as My Home, is a virtual personal space in Maple World that can be furnished and decorated by items obtained during gameplay and events. There are a handful of house types including time-limited event items.

To purchase a house, players must reach Level 200 (Level 260 in KoreaMS) and accept the quest [Home] A Place of Your Own. Once a player has purchased a house from the quest, they can follow up the questline in order to unlock more features.

Decorating a house is done through the use of furniture items and furnishings. These items can be obtained through various in-game events or activities. Furniture items can be placed and arranged in the house to create a unique and personalized place. Players can then invite their friends to visit their houses and show off their decorating skills. They can also visit their friends and random houses to see how they have decorated their spaces.

In addition to being a fun and creative side activity, the house system also has some practical benefits. Some furniture have functional features such as a storage system, Professions or General Store functions, which work with all characters in the world. Overall, the Home system is a nice side activity that allows players to express their creativity and flex what they have, as well as have a sense of ownership in the game world.

Punch Machine - Punching Game

Players (including visitors) can interact with the Punch Machine to trigger a mini-game. The player hits the punching bag and it records damage-per-minute. On its specific UI, the character's name, total damage dealt, and ranking is displayed.
Each attempt costs 10,000 mesos. Participation is limited to 10 players per day. Players must wait 10 minutes until they can attempt the mini-game again.





Gas Oven - Special

The Gas Oven is a special furnishing that allows players to bake items inside their house.
Interacting with the Gas Oven will begin the baking process. Baking takes 6 hours to complete. During this time, the player cannot leave their house or log off, or the process will be cancelled.
After 6 hours, players will receive one of the following:

  • Homemade Bread
  • Fresh Apple Pie

These items are usable and increase a random trait when used.

Maplemas Donation Kettle - Donation

Interacting with the Maplemas Donation Kettle will prompt players if they wish to donate mesos to Querencia. Note that players can only interact with a Maplemas Donation Kettle placed in their own Home.
Similar to other Donor quests, the player is given a 1-hour time limit to complete their donation and a chance to earn the Querencia Donor medal.
To obtain the medal, with a single donation, donate a larger amount of Mesos to the Maplemas Donation Kettle than anyone else in the same world during the given month. If the player successfully makes the highest donation for the month, they will be granted the medal.

Mysterious Treasure Chest - Password Game

The Mysterious Treasure Chest features a password guessing game designed for visitors.
The homeowner can place a reward item in the chest and set a secret password between 000-999. The game will be available for 10 minutes once a secret password is set. Visiting players can interact with the chest and attempt to guess the correct password. If the guess is correct, the visitor receives the reward set by the homeowner.
The chest must manually be reset by the homeowner in order to be used again.
Note that the Mysterious Treasure Chest only functions in Interactive Worlds.



Antique Wardrobe - Story

The Antique Wardrobe can be interacted with once a day to begin a choose-your-own-adventure style mini-game.
The game begins with the player selecting one of four items: Dagger, Encyclopedia, Boots, or Compass. Each item influences the path in the game.
The player begins the adventure with 100 HP and must survive five randomized events to complete the journey. Events may result in HP loss or grant benefical effects, with outcomes varying each run.
There are no rewards for successfully completing the mini-game, as it is purely for entertainment.

Caretaker's Support

Once a day, players may obtain a buff by talking to their home's caretaker. There are a total of 4 buffs available from the caretaker, and only one can be selected per day. The level of the buff depends on the caretaker's Closeness, with the lowest level being applied at 0 Closeness, and the level of the buff increasing every 20 Closeness, up to a maximum of level 6. The buff effects at 0, 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 closeness are as follows:

  • EXP Obtained: +5/6/7/8/9/10%
  • Boss Damage: +10/11/12/13/14/15%
  • Attack Power and Magic Attack: +15/18/21/24/27/30
  • Critical Rate: +10/12/14/16/18/20%

The caretaker buff is immediately applied upon obtainment, and lasts for 10 minutes. Every 5 Housework points on the caretaker will increase the duration of the caretaker's buff by 1 minute, up to a maximum of 30 minutes at 100 Housework. The stats of the home's caretaker may be increased daily by talking to the caretaker, or by giving them a gift.
