Battle Overview

Limbo can be fought in Normal and Hard mode. To deal regular damage to the boss, ⬢Sacred Power / Authentic Force (SAC / AUT) is required. Normal mode and Hard mode require a minimum of ⬢500 SAC / AUT for each phase. Story Mode requires a minimum of ⬢400 SAC / AUT for each phase. You will deal -10% final damage for every 10 SAC / AUT below the required amount; if you have 100 or more SAC / AUT below the required amount, you will only deal a total of 5% final damage. Similarly, you will deal +5% final damage for every 10 SAC / AUT above the required amount, up to +25% (50 above).

You must be in a party of 1 - 3 players in order to fight him. The battle has a time limit of 30 minutes, and a Potion Cooldown of 10 seconds is applied. All attacks deal % HP damage, so they must be manually avoided, as it ignores any avoidability stats, and damage reduction skills (unless the skill is specifically stated to include damage proportional to HP). There are 3 phases.

NOTE: Most damage values will vary based on difficulty, and will be listed in order from lowest difficulty to highest. Example: if listed as 25% / 30%, then the 25% correlates to Normal mode and the 30% correlates to Hard mode. If there are no listed values, then it is the same regardless of difficulty unless stated otherwise.

All Phases

Erosion

  • Erosion Value


Unlike other bosses with a death counter, each party member will instead receive a separate Erosion Value counter. You will start with a value of 0, and if it increases to 1,000, you will die and be kicked out of the fight. Most of Limbo's patterns will increase your Erosion by a certain amount, but certain patterns will decrease your Erosion. You will lose 150 Erosion if you die at any point during the battle.

  • Note that on the Party Member HP UI, the numbers are reversed, with your Erosion starting at 1,000 and being kicked out if it decreases to 0. This will not be referenced on this page when discussing the amount a pattern will increase or decrease your Erosion by, instead referencing the values starting at 0 and increasing up to 1,000.

Spirit Purification

  • Spirit Purification Gauge, filled halfway

All players are granted a skill called Spirit Purification, which is activated by pressing the NPC Chat / Interact / Harvest key similar to Will's Moonlight Gauge. It will gradually fill up over time, and is used to limit the Erosion you gain throughout the battle. Once the gauge is completely filled, you will want to use it immediately to lower the Risk Gauge. Most of Limbo's attacks will reduce the gauge upon getting hit, so you will have to play carefully.

The Erosion Buildup Gauge, also known as the Risk Gauge, is the bar above Spirit Purification, and will have a different effect based on the current boss phase. Your goal is to cast Spirit Purification to lower it as much as possible, as it will either directly or indirectly cause you to gain more Erosion the higher it is.

Phase 1

“Hahahahahaha! Beautiful, isn't it? You'll be part of it soon.”

― Phase 1's intro cutscene

The first phase will have you fighting in the Space of Replicated Truth, where you will battle Limbo's summoned Specters. Every 100 seconds, Limbo will perform Spatial Transition and swap the battlefield between Specter A and Specter B, with the timer shown below the Erosion Counter.

During this phase, the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge will start at 100%, being reduced by 10% every time you cast Spirit Purification. Once Limbo casts Spatial Transition, your Erosion will increase by 10 for every 10% remaining in the Erosion Buildup Gauge (e.g. +20 Erosion if the Erosion Buildup Gauge is at 20%. To mitigate this, you will want to avoid getting hit by Limbo's attacks as much as possible in order to fill the Spirit Purification.

  • Pillars: Throughout Phase 1, pillars will target you from the ceiling three times in a row, similar to Will's Spider Legs. Each hit will deal 20% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 10 / 15.

Specter A

Specter A is a Turtle Anima-themed Specter that surrounds himself in a shield bubble, which players must stay in to prevent their Erosion from increasing in the Erosion Area outside.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge by 10%, Spirit Purification will grant immunity to the effects of the Erosion Area for 5 seconds.
  • Erosion Area: If players leave the bubble and enter the Erosion Area for more than 2 seconds, black squares will appear throughout the map and increase your Erosion by 10, plus an additional 3 every second you are outside of the bubble. The black squares can also deal 15% / 20% HP damage and inflict Confusion for up to 2 seconds, reversing your key directions.
  • Abyssal Being: If you enter the Erosion Area, an eye effect will appear above your character that periodically deals 20% HP damage, inflicts Stun for up to 1 second, and applies Super Knockback.

Specter A's direct attacks are as follows:

  • Forward Attack: This is a two-part attack; Specter A will charge forward and slash his sword, then finish by slamming his shield behind him. Both hits can deal 30% HP damage, inflict Stun for up to 1 second, and apply Super Knockback. It can be avoided by standing behind him while he charges, and then moving in front of him when he uses his shield slam. This attack has a 10 second cooldown.
  • Backward Attack: The inverse of the previous attack; Specter A will charge backward and slam his shield, then finish by slashing his sword in front of him. Both hits can deal 30% HP damage, inflict Stun for up to 1 second, and apply Super Knockback. It can be avoided by standing in front of him while he charges, and then moving behind him when he uses his sword slash. This attack has a 10 second cooldown.
  • Horizontal Slash: Specter A will slash his sword horizontally, leaving an eye-shaped afterimage for a split second. Deals 20% HP damage and teleports you to a random spot on the map, but it can be avoided by ducking. This attack has a 10 second cooldown.
  • Barrier: This is a three-part attack; Specter A will create a barrier in the middle of him, dealing 20% HP damage and blocking players from passing. He will then slam his sword and shield away from the barrier, dealing 30% / 40% HP damage; this can be avoided by standing near the barrier. He will finish by destroying the barrier, dealing 30% / 40% HP damage; this can be avoided by standing away from the barrier. All three parts of this attack can apply Super Knockback. This attack has a 25 second cooldown.

Specter B

Specter B is a Snake Anima-themed Specter.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge by 10%, Spirit Purification will restore a destroyed portion of the map.
  • Platform Destruction: Every 30 seconds, Limbo will destroy a random portion of the map, leaving an opening for players to fall into. Occasionally, Specter B will also use a Laser attack that destroys the two portions to the left and right of him. Falling into an opening will increase your Erosion by 50.

Specter B's direct attacks are as follows:

  • Nightmare: Specter B will target a random player and fly up into the shape of a ball, descending downward at a marked location. Deals 50% / 60% HP damage. This attack has an 8 second cooldown.
  • Lasers: Specter B will fire lasers from his weapons to his left and right, destroying both of those portions of the map. Deals 40% HP damage and inflicts Stun for up to 2 seconds. This attack has a 60 second cooldown.
  • Creeping Terror: Specter B will burrow into the ground and become invincible, similar to Ark's Creeping Terror skill. A random player will have a debuff icon above their head, and Specter B will emerge from the ground after a few seconds, dealing 80% HP damage if the player is above him. If the platform below him is destroyed when he emerges, he will teleport to a nearby platform.. This attack has a 45 second cooldown.
  • Specter Summon: A small Specter will appear on the far left or the far right and bounce onto each platform across the map, jumping over any gaps left by destroyed platforms. Each hit deals 20% HP damage and applies Super Knockback, potentially pushing you back multiple times if you get caught by it. The small Specter can be avoided by jumping over or dashing under it at the right time. This attack has a 25 / 20 second cooldown.

Phase 2

“You're holding up well, hahahahaha! But there's plenty left in store!”

― Phase 2's intro cutscene

The second phase will have you fighting in the Space of Replicated Souls, where you will battle more of Limbo's summoned Specters. You will fight Specter C for the first half of the fight and Specter D for the second half, with a brief minigame in between the two.

During this phase, the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge will be replaced with the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge, starting at 0% and increasing by 4% every second. You will need to continuously use Spirit Purification to reduce the gauge and prevent it from filling up to 100%. The gauge reaching 100% will trigger a special pattern (depending on which Specter you are battling), rendering the boss invincible and potentially causing the party's Erosion Value to increase. Using Spirit Purification will reduce the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge as follows, depending on the party size, Specter, and difficulty.

Specter C

  • Soul Blossom (Left)
  • Soul Blossom (Right)
  • Powerful Soul Blossom (Left)
  • Powerful Soul Blossom (Right)

Specter C is a Specter that spawns alongside two Soul Blossoms. Initially, you will fight Specter C's Heart, which is its armless form. At certain points during the battle, it will absorb the Soul Blossoms and use them for arms, transforming into his basic armed form. If the special pattern is triggered, it will transform into Frenzied Specter C (armed form surrounded by a shield). Whenever Specter C uses the Soul Blossoms for arms, either during his basic armed form or his frenzied form, Spirit Purification cannot be used, but it will still be reduced if you get hit.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge, Spirit Purification will weaken the Soul Blossoms.
  • Soul Blossoms: There will be two Soul Blossoms, one on the far left and the far right. They will attack you if you move close to them, dealing 20% HP damage if you get hit. If the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge reaches 50%, they will grow in size with increased attack range and damage, dealing 40% / 50% HP damage and increasing your Erosion by 0 / 30.
  • Teardrops: A pool of teardrops appears at the top of the map, raining down slow-moving teardrops up to 5 times. These teardrops will deal 10% HP damage and inflict Slow for up to 2 seconds. If the special pattern is triggered, these teardrops will increase your Erosion by 10. This attack has a 16 / 14 second cooldown.
  • Chain Attack: This is a two-part attack; Specter C will create warning signs on the ground that launch you into the air, after which he uses an explosion attack. The warning signs will deal 15% HP damage, while the explosion attack will deal 35% / 40% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 50 / 80. To avoid this attack, stand on a safe spot in between the warning signs, or use a rush / dash skill on the ground to avoid being launched into the air. Magicians with teleport skills can also teleport down onto the ground before getting hit by the explosion attack. This attack has a 15 second cooldown.
  • Energy Explosion: Specter C will create either 1 or 2 mini explosions around the player. If he creates 1 explosion, it can be avoided by moving away from him. If he creates 2 explosions, it can be avoided by standing close to him. Getting hit by the explosions will deal 25% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 12. This attack has a 8 second cooldown.

At exactly 80% HP remaining for Phase 2, Specter C will immediately absorb the Soul Blossoms, becoming invincible for a few seconds and transforming into his armed form for 30 seconds. Afterward, he will become invincible again for a few seconds before transforming back into his armless form. He will transform into this form again after an additional 20% HP is depleted after he transforms back into his armless form; for example, if he transforms back into his armless form with 74.6% HP remaining, he will transform into his armed form again at 54.6% HP remaining, but he will not transform again if he transforms back into his armless form with 70% HP or less remaining. While in this form, he has the following attacks:

  • Sequential Explosion: Specter C will attack 6 spots in a row either from left to right or right to left, with each spot being about 1.5 characters apart. Getting hit will deal 30% HP damage and inflict Stun for up to 1 second. This attack has a 6 second cooldown.
  • Zone Plate Attack: Specter C will create 4 warning signs on the ground, with each dealing 25% HP damage. To avoid this attack, stand on a safe spot in between the warning signs. This attack has a 12 second cooldown.

If the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge reaches 100% and the special pattern is triggered, Specter C will transform into Frenzied Specter C (armed form surrounded by a shield), and become completely invincible for 30 seconds. During the special pattern, you will need to avoid all of his attacks:

  • Up and Down: Specter C will use 4 warning indicators, after which he will use 4 attacks that must be avoided by jumping or ducking depending on the indicator: a purple warning indicator means you will need to jump up, while a pink warning indicator means you will need to duck. After the 4 warning indicators, he will use the corresponding attacks with a short delay in between each: he uses an explosion for the attack where you must duck, and he will cover the ground with energy for the attack where you must jump. Each attack deals 25% HP damage and increases your Erosion by 50 / 80.
  • Left and Right: After the previous attack sequence, he will slam the ground with his Soul Blossoms 4 times either to the left or the right. It is recommended to stand in the center and avoid his attacks based on where he is about to strike. Each attack deals 30% HP damage and increases your Erosion by 50 / 80.

Shadow of the Source

Once Specter C is defeated and Phase 2 reaches 50% HP remaining, each party member will be moved to a separate planet map with spikes and the Shadow of the Source walking on the planet. Jumping on the spikes will push it to the opposite side of the planet. The goal is to jump on the spikes and hit the Shadow of the Source 3 times within 30 seconds. If you defeat it, your Erosion will be decreased by 150, but not less than 0. If you don't defeat the Shadow of the Source, you will simply be moved out of the map. After this test, you will begin the battle with Specter D.

Specter D

Specter D will be fought in a map with two floors. As the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge increases every 25%, Specter D will summon Abyssal Serpents that form Soul Walls, closing off certain sections of the map.

  • 25% Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge: Left side
  • 50% Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge: Right side
  • 75% Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge: Bottom
  • 100% Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge: Top

Any players standing in an area as it gets blocked off will take 300% HP damage. Upon the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge reaching 100%, the special pattern will be triggered.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge, Spirit Purification will remove some of the Soul Walls.
  • Teleport: Teleports to a random player. This has a 6 second cooldown.
  • Wave Barrier: Specter D will summon two waves on each side of him that move toward his left and right, slowly shrinking as they move towards the edges of the map. If Specter D is on the bottom of the map, these waves will initially be tall enough to hit players on the top platform. The waves will deal 40% / 45% HP damage every 0.2 seconds, inflict Slow for up to 2 seconds, and increase your Erosion by 30 / 50. This attack has a 20 second cooldown.
  • Predator's Teeth: Every 6 / 5 seconds, an eye-shaped mark will appear above a random player's head, which will turn into a horizontal or vertical tooth-shaped attack at the player's current location; if the eye's pupil is blue and horizontal, the teeth will be horizontal, while if the eye's pupil is purple and vertical, the teeth will be vertical. Touching the teeth will deal 15% HP damage for the first 3 seconds, while touching the teeth afterward will cause them to detonate and fire a laser in the direction the teeth are facing. The laser will deal 20% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 30 if hit. Additionally, Specter D will manually detonate any remaining teeth every 50 / 45 seconds, firing lasers in many directions.
  • Beast of the Abyss: Specter D will summon a beast above him that will attack his surrounding area, which is tall enough to hit players on the top platform. The beast will deal 40% / 45% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 10 / 30. If you are standing on the same platform as Specter D, you can avoid this attack by ducking. This attack has an 8 second cooldown.

If the Abyssal Aura / Risk Gauge reaches 100% and the special pattern is triggered, Specter D will send all players into the Endless Abyss for 14 seconds, which is a special map where the party must avoid getting hit by various spheres that will damage you and increase your Erosion. Movement skills will be disabled in this map, and your Speed and Jump will be fixed to 160 and 123 respectively. It is recommended to prevent the special pattern from activating, because it will increase your party's Erosion by a considerable amount.

  • Small Spheres: These will continuously appear in groups of 3 / 4 and follow you around, exploding if they get near you. They will deal 20% / 25% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 35 / 50.
  • Large Sphere: These will continuously appear in groups of 2 and bounce off the walls in patterns indicated by purple outlines. They will deal 35% / 40% HP damage, inflict Stun for up to 1 second, and increase your Erosion by 50 / 70.

Phase 3

“I will show you! The essence of the source! Despair before true power!”

― Phase 3's intro cutscene, version 1

“I will show you! The essence of the source! Despair before the truth!”

― Phase 3's intro cutscene, version 2

The third phase will send you into Limbo's Subconscious, where you will battle Limbo himself as he takes on a new smaller form. Similar to Phase 1, Limbo will perform Spatial Transition every 100 seconds and swap between his Dark form and his Light form, with the timer shown below the Erosion Counter. If the special pattern is triggered in either phase, the Spatial Transition timer will be paused until the special pattern ends.

Similar to Phase 1, the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge will start at 100%, being reduced by 10% every time you cast Spirit Purification. Once Limbo casts Spatial Transition, your Erosion will increase by 10 for every 10% remaining in the Erosion Buildup Gauge (e.g. +20 Erosion if the Erosion Buildup Gauge is at 20%. To mitigate this, you will want to avoid getting hit by Limbo's attacks as much as possible in order to fill the Spirit Purification.

  • Legs: During Phase 3, indicators will appear for 5 / 6 legs that will slam down on the map periodically. You can move away from or between the legs to avoid getting hit, with the white legs having a slightly shorter indicator. Each hit will deal 20% HP damage, inflict Stun for up to 1 second, and increase your Erosion by 0 / 10.

Dark / Black

Limbo's dark form is similar to the Phase 1 Specter A fight, where he surrounds himself in a shield bubble which players must stay in to prevent their Erosion from increasing in the Erosion Area outside.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge by 10%, Spirit Purification will grant immunity to the effects of the Erosion Area for 5 seconds and destroy 1 Polluted Area near you.
  • Polluted Area: A pink puddle will appear on the map every 7 seconds, as well as when hit by certain attack patterns. Standing on one will continuously deal 10% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 5. These can only be destroyed by using Spirit Purification next to one. If there are 4 - 5 Polluted Areas on the map at once, the screen will be surrounded with a glitchy border. If there are 6, the special pattern will be triggered.
  • Erosion Area: If players leave the bubble and enter the Erosion Area for more than 2 seconds, your Erosion will increase by 10, plus an additional 4 / 10 every second you are outside of the bubble. Additionally, 5 Mastermind Gluttons will spawn around you; getting caught by one will deal 25% HP damage and pull you away from Limbo for 2 seconds. Re-entering the bubble may decrease your Erosion.
  • Grasp of Truth: Limbo materializes two giant hands around himself to grasp onto you, dealing 20% / 25% HP damage and applying Super Knockback; can be avoided by crouching. This attack has an 8 second cooldown.
  • Dimensional Collapse: Limbo will collapse the outer bubble around him. Deals 30% / 35% HP damage, teleports you to a random spot on the map, and creates 1 Polluted Area; can be avoided by standing within the inner bubble. This attack has a 13 second cooldown.
  • Surging Shadow: Limbo will summon a materialize within his bubbles that will emerge from the ground to bite you. This attack deals 50% HP damage, creates 1 Polluted Area, and applies a debuff for 10 / 15 seconds upon getting hit. This debuff will increase your Erosion by 30 / 50 if you get hit by the Grasp of Truth or Dimensional Collapse during its duration. This attack has a 30 second cooldown.
  • Grab and Throw: A red-purple line appears in front of Limbo as he quickly charges into and grabs any players in his path, throwing them to the opposite side of the map. Getting grabbed and thrown will deal 20% HP damage and increase your Erosion by 50 / 100, while also rendering you defenseless; to avoid this attack, you must run behind or jump above him. This attack has a 23 second cooldown.

If 6 Polluted Areas appear on the map and the special pattern is triggered, Limbo will become completely invincible for 15 seconds, pausing the Spatial Transition timer. During the special pattern, Keira will generate a blue shield, which will shrink in size up to 5 times if you get hit by the legs that slam down on the map (dealing 30% HP damage) or by Limbo himself. Limbo will either attack you directly, dealing 25% / 30% HP damage and applying Super Knockback (5 second cooldown) or teleport to your location (6 second cooldown). It is recommended to lure Limbo away until the special pattern is near its end.

Once the pattern ends, a special screen effect will occur of Limbo biting down. Staying within the shield will decrease your Erosion by 50, but failure will increase your Erosion by 100. It is recommended to prevent the special pattern from activating due to Limbo's invincibility.

Light / White

Limbo's light form is similar to the Phase 2 Specter D fight, where he will continuously create Soul Walls to close off the edges of the map.

  • Spirit Purification: In addition to reducing the Erosion Buildup / Risk Gauge by 10%, Spirit Purification will remove some of the Soul Walls on the map. Note that you don't need to stand near the Soul Walls to remove them.
  • Soul Walls: Similar to Specter D, Soul Walls will continuously appear on alternating sides of the map, closing in from the left and the right similarly to Phase 1 of the Black Mage battle. Any players standing in an area as it gets blocked off will take 300% HP damage.
    • If you run into a Soul Wall, you will take 15% / 20% HP damage and a white sphere will appear that bounces between the walls for 5 seconds. Getting hit by the sphere will deal 20% / 25% HP damage and apply the Soul Mark debuff for 6 seconds, increasing the amount of Erosion gained by Limbo's Forward Attack and Monster of Truth by an additional 20.
    • If there are 4 - 5 Soul Walls on the map at once, the screen will be surrounded with a glitchy border. If there are 6 (3 on each side), the special pattern will be triggered.
  • Forward Attack: Limbo charges an attack for a couple seconds and a monster appears to attack you, dealing 20% / 30% HP damage, applying Super Knockback, and increasing your Erosion by 0 / 30 (20 / 50 if afflicted with the Soul Mark debuff). This attack has a 6 second cooldown.
  • Monster of Truth: Limbo will summon a large monster behind him that swims from one end of the map to the other, slowly sinking as it progresses. The monster will deal 30% HP damage every 0.2 seconds and increase your Erosion by 30 / 60 (50 / 80 if afflicted with the Soul Mark debuff). It is recommended to either use an invincibility skill or jump into the air and use Blink to avoid getting hit. This attack has a 28 second cooldown.

If 6 Soul Walls appear on the map and the special pattern is triggered, Limbo will become completely invincible for 15 seconds, pausing the Spatial Transition timer. During the special pattern, legs will continuously slam down on the map alongside blue water droplets. Getting hit by the legs will deal 30% HP damage, while getting hit by the water droplets will generate a blue shield that increases in size if you collect more water droplets. Limbo himself will either attack you directly, dealing 25% / 30% HP damage and applying Super Knockback (5 second cooldown) or teleport to your location (6 second cooldown). It is recommended to lure Limbo away until the special pattern is near its end.

Once the pattern ends, a special screen effect will occur of Limbo biting down. Staying within the shield will decrease your Erosion by 50, but failure will increase your Erosion by 100. It is recommended to prevent the special pattern from activating due to Limbo's invincibility.
