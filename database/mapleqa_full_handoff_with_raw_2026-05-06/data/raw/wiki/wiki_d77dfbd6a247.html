This massive whale-shaped submarine makes its berth in the strait between Henesys and Ellinia.
Its captain is Kyrin, a quarter-elf who has traveled the world in pursuit of Black Mage. She's recently settled on Victoria Island to train a new generation of Pirates.

Nautilus is an area in southeastern Victoria Island. The harbor serves as the second port city of Victoria Island, where the eponymous submarine is anchored. The Nautilus itself is a submarine that looks like a whale with a skull attached to the front. The Nautilus has a captain, Kyrin, who has returned to Victoria Island to inform the people of Victoria Island that the Black Mage is coming, and that they must prepare for the worst. She is recruiting an army of specially trained pirates to aid beginning pirates become stronger, so that they will be ever prepared. Explorer Beginners can choose to become Pirates here by talking to Kyrin in the Navigation Room. The Cooking with Tangyoon Party Quest is located near this area.

The city can be accessed on foot via Six Path Crossway, as well as via the Regular Cab in Victoria available in all major Victoria Island towns. Orange Mushrooms and Pigs are the only monster species in the vicinity, found on the Pasture / Beach path leading to the Nautilus. The Pasture is connected to Henesys via the Farm owned by Evan's family.

Trivia

  • Nautilus is named after the Nautilus, a submarine from the book Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea by Jules Verne.
  • Prior to the You & I / Rising Heroes Update and after the Legends Update, this area had a section to the east of town called the Beach, which contained an additional set of maps consisting of Pigs, Starfish, and Jellyfish monsters. These maps would also lead to Florina Beach, which was previously only accessible via the NPC Pason. With the You & I / Rising Heroes Update, the Beach was closed, and a single Beach map was added near the Pasture consisting of Ribbon Pigs and Blue Ribbon Pigs.
