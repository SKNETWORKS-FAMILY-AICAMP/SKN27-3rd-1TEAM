Quick Move

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

The Quick Move UI is a dropdown menu that appears underneath the mini map in major towns. The menu allows players to quickly talk to certain NPCs or move to certain areas.

Options

Icon NPC Description

Monster Park
Monster Park Shuttle 		Move to Monster Park.
Available at Level 105+

Ardentmill
Ardentmill Entrance 		Move to the town of professions, Ardentmill.

Legion Coin Shop
Squire Pancho Sanza 		Purchase various items from the Legion Coin Shop.

Crystals & Elixirs
Collector 		Sell Intense Power Crystals or purchase various elixirs.

Maple Coin Exchange[1]
Trader 		You can exchange 4 types of Maple Coins (Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond) for Mesos.

Auction House
Eggrich 		Use the Auction House.

Meso Market
Miss Richfield 		Exchange Maple Points and Mesos.

Cube Shop
Heroic Cube Shop 		Glowing Cubes and Bright Cubes are available for purchase.[2]

Dimensional Mirror
Dimensional Mirror 		Select and enter Grand Athenaeum or Party Quests.
Available at Level 50+

Symbol Express Pass
Spiegelmann 		Use Spiegelmann's Symbol Express Pass.
Available at Level 200+

Maple Administrator
Maple Administrator 		Talk with the Maple Administrator.

Storage Room
Storage 		Use the Storage Room.

Wedding Village
Nana K 		You can visit the Wedding Village by talking to Nana K.[3]
