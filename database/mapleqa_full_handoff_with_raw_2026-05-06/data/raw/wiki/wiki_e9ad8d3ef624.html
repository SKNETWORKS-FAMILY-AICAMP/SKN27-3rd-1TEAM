Removed Content / Feature
This article contains information no longer in-game, and are thus historical records of MapleStory.

Peach Blossom Island was an East Asia-themed wedding area added to ChinaMS in November 2005, eventually being added to TaiwanMS and MapleSEA, Peach Blossom island contained the first wedding system in the game, predating Amoria. Players could travel to the island by talking to one of the monks in either Henesys, Orbis, or Ludibrium. From there, a couple would need to pay 100,000 Mesos to enter the wedding hall, where the ceremony would be held. At the end of the wedding, the couple would be given wedding rings and be sent to a Banquet Room. Any couples that wished to divorce would need to pay 1,000,000 Mesos to enter the divorce hall, where the couple would both lose their wedding rings. It was in ChinaMS, TaiwanMS, and MapleSEA, but it was removed from the game in favor of Amoria.

In ChinaMS, existing players who have equipped Engagement Ring cannot equip it again after it's unequipped.
