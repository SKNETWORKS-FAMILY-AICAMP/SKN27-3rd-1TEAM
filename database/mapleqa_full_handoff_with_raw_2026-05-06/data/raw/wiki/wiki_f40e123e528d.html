This page will list all monsters in Monster Park, in the order of the area they appear, rather than by level. These monsters do not give normal EXP; instead, defeating these monsters will accumulate experience that you will receive when you complete your Monster Park run (with the exception of Monster Park Extreme, which provides a specific amount based on your character's level). Note that these experience values will not increase during EXP increasing events or via EXP increasing buffs, but on Sundays, you will be able to receive 1.5 times the EXP.
