Leftovers are items dropped by monsters and reflect on the nature of the monster (i.e. Snail drops Green Snail Shell and Orange Mushroom drops Orange Mushroom Cap). In some quests a specific number of certain leftovers are needed to help complete the quest. Leftovers can also be sold to NPC's, which may be profitable.
