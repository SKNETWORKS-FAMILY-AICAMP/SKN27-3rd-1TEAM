Cube

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Cubes are usable items that reset the potential and bonus potential of your equipment. Cubes can be obtained in the Cash Shop using Mesos in Heroic worlds, NX in Interactive worlds, as well as from event shops or crafted using the Smithing Profession. Each cube has a random chance to change the potential lines, or to increase the potential rank, but will not increase the number of potential lines. Some cubes can also only be used on untradable equipment.

In KoreaMS, potential can be instead reset using Mesos at a fixed cost based on the equipment level and potential rank. Cubes are generally unavailable for purchase and are only given during certain events.

Trivia

  • In KoreaMS, all potential cubes were removed in the Dreamer Update, while all bonus potential cubes were removed in the Milestone Update.
    • Event cubes, generally Karma versions of the previously available cubes, remain available and continue to be obtained via events.
    • In the Crown Update, Karma Black and White Cubes are available via MAPLE Daily Gift and Reward Points Shop.
  • Cubes were revamped under different names in GlobalMS (v239), MapleSEA (v225), and ChinaMS (v211)
    • In GlobalMS v239, probabilities of changing potential tiers were also changed.
  • In MapleSEA v234, a potential tier upgrade benefit system was introduced for cash cubes.
