Wind Archer or Wind Breaker is a Cygnus Knight Bowman released during the v.73: Cygnus Summer Knights[5] Update.

Blessed by the spirit of wind, they use nature and wind based attacks. They are taught by and answer to the Chief Knight of Wind, Irena the Wind Archer, for whom they are named.

Like Bow Master, Wind Archer consumes Arrows for Bow and receives an attack boost from the first arrow in Use Inventory.
