This mysterious temple, where worshippers gather to honor Rhinne, the Transcendent of Time, floats above the Minar Forest. The past, present, and future all converge here, and one can visit them through three special doors.

“The temple dedicated to Rhinne, the Transcendent of Time, where she is worshipped as a goddess. This mysterious place floats above the Minar Forest, and its three gates to the past, present, and future reveal that all time periods coexist here.”

― In-game description for Temple of Time

Temple of Time is one of the seven Ossyrian subcontinents, and is where the past, present, and future all exist within. The Goddess of Time, Rhinne, resides here. Temple of Time encompasses the towns of Temple of Time (aka Three Doors), Crack in Time, and Gate to the Future (consisting of Henesys Ruins and Twilight Perion). This region can be accessed on foot by talking to Corba at Leafre's Station and transforming into a Baby Draco to fly to the temple. The three doors from left to right lead to Gate to the Future, Arcane River, and Gate to the Past, the latter of which is the main area. In addition, there is a Crack in Time on the right side of town that leads to past versions of Leafre and the Dimensional Schism, where Arkarium is hiding.

Completing the Pink Bean questline will unlock access to the Pink Bean Boss Battle for all characters within the same world. Defeating Pink Bean allows players to obtain the following:

  • The Eye Accessory  Black Bean Mark, which is part of the Boss Accessory Set
  • The Belt  Golden Clover Belt, which is part of the Boss Accessory Set
  • The Pocket Item  Pink Holy Cup, which is part of the Boss Accessory Set

Towns / Regions

Temple of Time

Temple of Time's town is where the Guardians of Time reside and maintain the temple. In addition, the It is divided into three sections: Memory Lane, Road of Regrets, and Road to Oblivion. However, one can only access all sections of the temple after completing a series of quests given by the Temple Keeper. The Road to Oblivion also contains a Star Force area called the Detour to Oblivion, consisting of four maps which require 90 Star Force or higher, but these maps can only be accessed after completing the Pink Bean questline or by using a teleport. The Major Boss Pink Bean can be fought here. In addition, a few field bosses can be encountered during the questline: Dodo, a floating whale that feasts on memories, Lilynouch, an Ice Knight with frozen emotions, and Lyka, an oxen criminal that was sentenced to live its days in the Road to Oblivion. The portal on the right side of town leads to the Crack in Time where players can access past versions of Leafre and the Temple of Time.

Crack in Time

Main article: Crack in Time

The Crack in Time was created when Arkarium traveled into past in an attempt to kill the Demon, with the goal of preventing him and the Heroes from weakening and sealing the Black Mage. The Silent Crusade has sent you to the Temple of Time to track Arkarium and thwart his plans. The area contains a past version of Leafre and the Temple of Time from before the Black Mage's sealing a few centuries prior. Past versions of a few Heroes can be found here, namely Aran with Maha, Mercedes, and Freud with Afrien. Note that teleportation items and skills do not work in this area. Players can fight the Major Boss Arkarium here.

Gate to the Future

Main article: Gate to the Future

The Gate to the Future, which is normally not open to outsiders, has started to exude dark energy. Entering will present one with dystopian future versions of Henesys, Ereve, and Perion. These are later revealed to be illusions conjured up by Lucid.

Story

For information on the full story, visit this page. Please note that this story page only covers the Gate of the Past. For information on the Gate to the Future, visit this page.

Pink Bean

An Alliance member arrived at the Temple of Time and learned from the Temple Keeper that an intruder had broken into the Gate of the Past. The traveler agreed to help catch the intruder, and so the Temple Keeper gave them his blessings as a representative of Rhinne. On their journey, the traveler had a portion of their memories consumed by Dodo, the Whale of Time. In order to recover them, they met with the Memory Keeper, who told them that in order to regain their memories of their innocent, youthful memories of their journey's beginning, they would need to speak with the one who had helped them at the start of their adventure. After recovering their memories, they proceeded down the Road of Regrets, where their emotions were frozen by Lilynouch the Ice Knight, preventing them from feeling the regret that they needed to make it past the Road of Regrets. In order to thaw their emotions, they met with the Sorcerer and purchased a potion that allowed them to restore their emotions.

At the end of the Road to Oblivion, they defeated Lyka, the Guardian of the Temple, but found the path to the deepest sanctum blocked. They spoke with the Record Keeper, who confirmed that the Goddess' Promise of oblivion - the ability to forget - still held, meaning that there should be no reason why the path should be closed to them. The Memory Keeper realized that the intruder must have created a barrier, and so the traveler collected the materials for the Temple Keeper to break the barrier. They proceeded ahead and entered the Dusk Temple in the Twilight of Gods, representing the temple at its earliest point in history. There, they encountered Kirston attempting to use the Mirror of the Goddess to physically move the past and summon the Black Mage. However, a strange energy was emitted by the mirror, causing a monster named Pink Bean to appear. Pink Bean used its power to cause the statues of the old gods to come to life, though the traveler defeated Pink Bean and forced it back through the mirror, leaving Kirston's whereabouts unknown.

Kao

While exploring the Temple of Time, the Alliance member encountered an amnesiac Temple Keeper wandering through the Gate of the Past. The Alliance member worked with the other Temple Keepers in order to help the amnesiac Temple Keeper regain their memories. As a result of their efforts, the Temple Keeper was able to remember their name: Kao. To the Alliance member's surprise, Kao removed their hood and revealed that their face was identical to the Alliance member's.
