Transformation Item

From the MapleStory Wiki, a knowledge base about everything MapleStory
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Transformation Items are usable items that temporarily change a player's appearance by transforming them into a different form, such as a monster or NPC. The transformation effect lasts for a limited duration and will automatically end once the buff expires or if the player cancels the effect.
