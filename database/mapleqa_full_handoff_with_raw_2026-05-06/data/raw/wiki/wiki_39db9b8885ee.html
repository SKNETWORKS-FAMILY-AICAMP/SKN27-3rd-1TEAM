Removed Content / Feature
This article contains information no longer in-game, and are thus historical records of MapleStory.

Star Planet was a cross-world area where players Level 33 and above could play a variety of mini-games, rewarding Star Points to improve one's Star Grade, as well as Reward Points. While in Star Planet, certain features, Skills, and Inventory menus (Use and Etc tabs) were not be available. Players could use the Star Planet Limousine or the elevators to travel between the Central Plaza and the Star Arena Street. The following minigames were available, with some available on release and others added in later updates:

  • Attack! Typing Defense (1 player)
  • Mayhem! Typing Defense (3 players)
  • Win Streak! Rock-Paper-Scissors (1 player)
  • Star One Card (4 players)
  • Star Monsteropoly (4 players)
  • Star Game of Yut / Supernatural Yut (2 players)
  • Star Step (2 - 5 players)
  • The Legends Return! (3 vs 3 players)

The following world events were also available:

  • Star Bingo (up to 100 players)
  • 1 vs 100 Rock-Paper-Scissors (up to 100 players)
  • Celebrity Photo Op
  • Lucky Ticket

Players could also receive 2x EXP Coupons every day by accumulating Star Points and filling your EXP buff gauge, granting a 15, 30, 45, or 60-minute buff depending on your gauge's progress.

Star Planet was removed in all servers in 2016, mostly due to lack of participation and abuse by illegitimate players. However, some mini-games introduced in Star Planet were reintroduced in later events seasonally, such as the Legends Return, One Card, and Yut.

Trivia

  • Several of the human NPCs were reused as Spectators and Fan Club Members in Kerning Tower. One of the characters from the Busy Couple NPC was reused as a Groupie.
  • One Card is based on Uno, a American shedding-type card game.
  • Yut is based on Yunnori, a traditional Korean board game.
