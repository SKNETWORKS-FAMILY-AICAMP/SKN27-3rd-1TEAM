Sia Astelle is a Shine Magician released during the Stargazer[3] Update in GMS. Originally, Sia was exclusive to MapleStory M.

For Sia only, 6th Job skills are unlocked and enhanced using Erda Link[4] instead of HEXA Matrix.

Trivia

  • Her leitmotif/theme BGMs are Star Guardian and Falter mind became a starlight.
  • For some quests with job specific dialogues, Sia and Explorers' dialogues are the same[5].
  • Only Female option is available at character creation.
  • Starting Alchiba and Vega Sitting in a Tree, which teleports the player near Ellinia, is required to access areas outside of Oort.
