This article contains information no longer in-game, and are thus historical records of MapleStory.

Veracent was an area created by EuropeMS. The original incarnation was for players Level 65 and above, but it was overhauled later on to be Level 100 and above, with the number of monsters being reduced. It consisted of alternate versions of mobs from throughout Maple World (with the prefix "Dark" added to each mob). The main area of attention was Vampire Castle, which contained a party play zone consisting of monsters with high HP and EXP. The two final bosses were Claude, a vampire related to the storyline, and Kerberos, the three-headed dog from hell.

Players were able to obtain special equipment from Veracent as well, namely Darkness equipment and Beginning/Intermediate/High Knight Sets in the original version and the Evasion Set in the revamped version.

It was available in EuropeMS (and removed when it merged with GlobalMS), ChinaMS, TaiwanMS, and MapleSEA, but it has been removed from all servers.
