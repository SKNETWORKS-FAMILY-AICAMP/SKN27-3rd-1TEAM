Mu Lung Dojo is a solo Dungeon located in Mu Lung Garden's Mu Lung region. This Dungeon can be started by talking to So Gong in Mu Lung Dojo Hall; it can be accessed via the Maple Guide, as well as by clicking on Mu Lung Dojo Bulletin Board in Mu Lung Temple.

The Mu Lung Dojo is a test where players can test their skills on endurance and power by continuously battling bosses. Any damage dealt to enemies inside Mu Lung Dojo will be reduced by 90%, meaning you will deal 10% of your normal damage. Two separate categories exist for rankings: Novice for players between Level 105 - 200, and Master for Level 201+ players. Your rank will be determined based on time spent in the Dojo and the highest floor you reached. Based on your rank, you can receive Mu Lung Dojo Points, as well as gloves that grant special potential bonuses.

Details

Upon entering Mu Lung Dojo, all of your buffs will be reset and you will be sent to the Mu Lung Dojo Atrium, where you will be given 2 minutes to prepare your buffs. Once you begin, consumable items cannot be used inside certain Mu Lung Dojo Floors, except Floors 10, 20, 30, and 40 - 100, where consumables will have a 15 second cooldown. However, MP recovery will be much higher inside the Dojo. After each boss is defeated, the timer will pause until 10 seconds have passed or until you advance to the next floor, allowing players to prepare for the next floor.

Rewards

The rankings will be counted between Wednesday at 11:30 PM Server Time and Thursday at 12:30 AM Server Time, during which players will be restricted from entering the Dojo. Upon completion of the rankings, players will receive special gloves, titles, and Dojo Points (discussed below).

Dojo Points

Every floor you clear will give 10 Dojo Points, while every 10th floor you clear will grant 110 Dojo Points. You will also gain a larger amount of points based on your ranking (calculated by level tier as well as highest floor cleared). To receive ranking points, you must be in the Top 50% of the Novice group or Top 70% of the Master group. These points can be used to purchase various goods from Lao's shop. Some of the more notable rewards include:

  • Mu Gong's Honor EXP Guarantee: This will extract 10,000 Honor EXP from yourself and grant you a tradable Mu Gong's Guaranteed Medal of Honor, which can be given or sold to another person/character.
  • Unity Training Center Entrance Charms: These come in durations of 30 minutes, as well as 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, or 24 hours, and are used to access the Mu Lung Dojo Unity Training Center, a map where you gain a small amount of EXP every 5 seconds.

Fog Forest Training Grounds

Deep within Mu Lung is the Fog Forest Training Grounds, a secret training area where sages go to hone their skills. So Gong has opened this area to those who meet the following requirements:

  • Level 200 and above
  • Characters with a record of clearing Mu Lung Dojo Floor 30 or higher

Players can enter this training area in a party of 1 or more by talking to So Gong at Mu Lung Dojo Hall. Players can stay in the Fog Forest Training Area up to 1 hour at a time, up to 5 times every day. Once inside, you can use the UI to change various settings and recreate the battle scenario of your choice.

The Monster Settings are as follows:

  • Type: Normal Monster / Boss Monster
  • Level: 1 - 300
  • Defense: 0 - 500
  • Size: Small / Medium / Large
  • Normal Monster HP:
    • 5,000,000,000 (5 billion)
    • 10,000,000,000 (10 billion)
    • 30,000,000,000 (30 billion)
    • 50,000,000,000 (50 billion)
    • 150,000,000,000 (150 billion)
    • 500,000,000,000 (500 billion)
    • Unlimited
  • Boss Monster HP:
    • 100,000,000,000 (100 billion)
    • 1,000,000,000,000 (1 trillion)
    • 10,000,000,000,000 (10 trillion)
    • 100,000,000,000,000 (100 trillion)
    • 1,000,000,000,000,000 (1 quadrillion)
    • Unlimited
  • Elemental Damage Taken: Reduction / No Reduction

One of the following Map Requirements can optionally be added:

  • Star Force: 1 - 140
  • Arcane Power: 1- 1,320
  • Sacred Power: 1 - 400

Up to 10 monsters can exist on the map at once. Players can click the Dismiss Previous button to remove the most recently summoned monster, or the Dismiss All button to remove all monsters.

Other perks including being unable to die inside the training grounds and a button to fully restore HP and MP. Players can also use the same exclusive buffs available within Boss Practice Mode, applying certain buffs for free while inside the training grounds without needing to consume your own buffs. Previously applied buffs of the same type will be removed upon using the Fog Forest Training Ground counterparts, and any restrictions that exist for the normal versions of these buffs will also apply to the Fog Forest Training Ground counterparts. The buffs included are as follows:

  • Skill Buffs: Noblesse Guild Skills, Heaven's Door, Hero's Echo, Weapon Tempering / Advanced Weapon Tempering
  • Potion Buffs: Sparkling Gold Star Potion, Sparkling Blue Star Potion, Sparkling Red Star Potion, Advanced Boss Rush Boost Potion, Advanced Great Hero Boost Potion, Advanced Penetrating Boost Potion, Advanced Great Blessing Potion, Legendary Hero Potion, Legendary Blessing Potion, Legendary Fortitude Potion, Legendary Insight Potion
  • Other Item Buffs: Sayram's Elixir, Masarayu's Gift Atmospheric Effect, Greater Blessing of the Guild / Blessing of the Guild, Blessing of the 275 / Blessing of the 250, Legion's Might, Extreme Red Potion, Extreme Green Potion, Extreme Blue Potion, Advanced Strength Potion X, Advanced Dexterity Potion X, Advanced Intelligence Potion X, Advanced Luck Potion X

Unity Training Center

Players who obtain a Unity Training Center Entrance Charm can enter the Mu Lung Dojo Unity Training Center, a map where you gain a small amount of EXP every 5 seconds. These charms come in durations of 30 minutes, as well as 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, or 24 hours. If you leave the map while you have time remaining, you will receive a lower-tier charm than what you initially started with to account for your remaining time.
