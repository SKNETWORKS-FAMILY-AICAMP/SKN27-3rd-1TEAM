Battle Overview

Magnus can be fought in Easy, Normal, and Hard mode. You must be in a party of 1 - 6 players in order to fight him, and each player will have a Death Count of 5 in Easy mode, 10 in Normal mode, and 15 in Hard mode. If all players reach 0 lives, then the battle will end and the party will be kicked out of the boss fight. The battle has a time limit of 30 minutes, and a Potion Cooldown of 5 seconds is applied.

Magnus is accompanied by a surrounding blue aura that shrinks as the battle goes on. Any players outside of the blue aura will have their damage and potion efficiency reduced. In Easy mode, damage will be reduced by 50% with no reduction to potion efficiency; in Normal mode, damage will be reduced by 90% and potion efficiency will be reduced by 20%; in Hard mode, damage will be reduced by 90% and potion efficiency will be reduced by 40%. Additionally, players will take 10% HP damage every 2 seconds outside of the blue aura.

NOTE: Most damage values will vary based on difficulty, and will be listed in order from lowest difficulty to highest. Example: if listed as 20% / 25% / 30%, then the 20% correlates to Easy mode, the 25% correlates to Normal mode, and the 30% correlates to Chaos mode. If there are no listed values, then it is the same regardless of difficulty unless stated otherwise.

Magnus has the following abilities:

  • Spin Slash: Magnus spins in place, dealing 30% HP damage in Easy mode and % HP damage equivalent to (100% HP - Player Defense) × 0.55 in Normal and Hard mode. This attack applies Super Knockback, and can be avoided by ducking. This attack has a 12 second cooldown.
  • Clone Summon: Magnus summons green clones of himself that rain down on various parts of the battlefield, dealing 30% / 100% / 100% HP damage. This attack has a 10 second cooldown.
  • Rush Slash: Magnus rushes forward to attack the player, dealing normal damage in Easy mode and % HP damage equivalent to (70% HP - Player Defense) × 0.55 in Normal and Hard mode. This attack has an 8 second cooldown.
  • Strength Release: Magnus envelops himself with his wings and prepares a blast that fires diagonally downwards on both of his sides, dealing 50% / 100% / 100% HP damage. This attack has a 15 second cooldown.
  • Slam Down: Magnus charges his sword with flames and crashes directly downward, dealing 50% / 100% / 100% HP damage. This attack has a 15 second cooldown.
  • Slow: Magnus covers an area in front of him, dealing 1% HP damage and inflicting players with Slow for up to 4 seconds; this Slow debuff seals all mobility-related skills like Teleport, Flash Jump, and dashing skills. This attack has a 120 second cooldown.
  • Zombify (Normal and Hard mode only): Magnus covers an area behind of him, dealing 1% HP damage and inflicting players with Zombify, turning players Undead and reducing potion efficiency by 50%, for up to 60 seconds. This attack has a 60 second cooldown.
  • Sleeping Gas: Magnus can release poisonous gas at three specific spots on the battlefield near the left and right edges of the map, indicated via markers on the ground. Getting hit by the gas will stun players for 5 seconds and block all recovery.
  • Meteors: Three different-colored meteors rain over the battlefield throughout the fight, which can be dodged with Dark Sight.
    • The small green meteors will deal 10% / 50% / 50% HP damage.
    • The medium blue meteors will deal 30% / 60% / 80% HP damage.
    • The large purple meteors will deal 50% / 80% / 100% HP damage.

The fight is separated into 4 stages, each one lasting for 25% of his HP. Entering a new stage will be marked with the message "Magnus can no longer suppress Guwaru. Guwaru's power grows..." When this happens, the amount of meteors that fall will increases, Sleeping Gas will appear more frequently, and his blue aura will shrink a little bit more. In Easy mode, the aura will not shrink to its 25% stage.
