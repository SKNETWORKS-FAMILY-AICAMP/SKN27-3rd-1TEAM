Monster Familiar Cards and their respective cards are copies of MapleStory Monsters that can be summoned through Roro the Familiar Manager. They can be obtained through Booster Packs in the Cash Shop or as drops by the monster they summon. The Familiars give effects and can attack when summoned, and have different ranks denoted by color of the Familiar icon's border.

This page will include Rare-tier Familiar Cards. The Monster Familiars in red either do not drop from the respective monster, or the monster cannot be accessed anymore (in some cases being obtainable via Booster Packs).
