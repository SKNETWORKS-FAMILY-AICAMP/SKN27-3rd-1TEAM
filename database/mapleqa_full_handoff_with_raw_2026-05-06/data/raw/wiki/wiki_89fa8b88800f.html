“A ruined land scarred by a High Flora invasion in the past. The natives have long since left this desert, and even the remaining High Flora base has seen better days.”

― In-game description for Verdel

Verdel is a satellite / moon in Grandis, located in the Western Front of the realm. Centuries ago, Verdel was invaded by the High Flora during their war of conquest, during which Brigadier General Limbo attempted to harvest the bioenergy of the moon and turn the native species of Verdel into Specters. His efforts were thwarted by Ark, who grew disillusioned by the war and attempted to warn the enemy of the imminent High Flora counterattack. Ark's betrayal was quickly discovered by Limbo, who attempted to turn Ark into a Specter. However, the ritual was interrupted by Ark's friend, Albaire, resulting in a large vortex that forced the High Flora to retreat. Centuries later, the strength of the vortex stranded a group of interstellar nomads, who attempted to find a way to escape. Ark characters start here upon creation, with their introductory questline centered around working with the caravan of nomads to escape Verdel. Throughout the storyline, Ark slowly recovers his memories about his time as a soldier and confronts Albaire, who has returned to Verdel on Limbo's orders to harvest the bioenergy of the moon.

This region cannot be accessed by foot, although it is possible to teleport there through the Maple Guide. It is divided into four sections: Refuge Outskirts, Sandstorm Zone, Desert Cavern, and Ravaged Base Remnants. While most classes can access these maps without doing quests, Ark characters slowly gain access to each section as they progress through their introductory questline.
