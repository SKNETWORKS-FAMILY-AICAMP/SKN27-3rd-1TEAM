Assemble (or Every Little Thing Every Precious Thing in GMS) is an update following NEXT. The update includes a new Anima Warrior class, Ren, new HEXA Skill Node, Ascent Skills, as well as a new Level 270+ boss First Adversary. The update also includes many user experience and quality of life improvements, including automatic AP distribution, various boss battle improvements, as well unification of cash inventory.

There are regional differences between the rollout of the update. In ChinaMS, TaiwanMS and JapanMS, the update also includes job revamps to Kanna and Hayato. In GlobalMS, the update includes the Mystic Frontier system, an expansion of the Familiar System, as well as various exclusive quality of life improvements. In MapleSEA, the update includes Blood Moon Forest and a new job, Mo Xuan.

Original (KoreaMS)

1st: Red-Eyed Wanderer, Ren and QoL Improvements

  • New Job: Ren, the Red-Eyed Wanderer, a Rabbit Anima Warrior
  • New Area: Vallora, the starting area for Ren
  • Character improvements
    • New job story quest skip feature, allowing players to skip early job stories and immediately starting at Level 30 (Level 120 for Zero, with Chapter 2 being auto-completed)
    • New menu option for automatic AP distribution for all jobs at each level up and job changes
      • Explorer Pirates cannot use this option until the 2nd Job Advancement
      • Xenon cannot use this option if STR, DEX, and LUK are all less than 330
    • New Buff Sequence system, allowing up to 30 consumables and skills to be added
    • Zero characters can use Tera Burninators
  • Item improvements and changes
    • New Collector's Elixir offering additional buffs similar to certain party buff skills including Combat Orders and Meditation
    • Usage of Meso, EXP, and drop rate increase potions can be used alongside other potions
    • Double Sacred Symbols obtained from selector coupons
    • Increased Spell Traces and Medals of Honor when defeating Elite Monsters
    • Removal of cooldown of Blessing of the Guild and Greater Blessing of the Guild
    • Removal of Pendant of the Spirit preparation time
    • Removal of message requirement for certain weather atmosphere buffs
    • MAPLE Daily Gift Day 8 reward changed to a selection box between a Mega Burninator or 3 VIP Boosters
    • Crafted Nodestones from Node Shards no longer tradable but remain transferable within characters of the same world via storage, and expiry set to 7 days
  • Dynamic field map reorganization
    • Level 60+ maps made instanced up to 4 players (8 players in Challenger World) per map, excluding tutorial, Mirror World, FriendStory, and Grand Athenaeum maps
    • New Treasure Hunter content, replacing Bounty Hunter Portals
      • When defeating level range monsters, a Gold or Diamond Treasure Box can drop on a set probability
      • Treasure Boxes appear 10 times a day, and Diamond Treasure Boxes only appear at monsters at Level 230+
      • Boxes can be opened via attacks, offering EXP with varying grades and amounts depending on the highest level monster in the field,
      • Removal of Bounty Hunter Portals and admission tickets
        • Monster collection related to these portals can be registered via purchasing Bounty Hunter Monsterblooms
        • Medals related to these portals can be registered at a low chance via quests
    • New Rune of Blessings, where players receive +200% EXP buff and the rune periodically attacks nearby monsters
      • After activating a rune 4 times, this new rune may appear
      • Odds of this rune appearing increases in the next cycle if it does not appear in this cycle
      • Certain cycles guarantee the appearance of this rune
  • Monster Park improvements
    • Dungeon type reorganized to Beginner Dungeon (Levels 105 to 200), Intermediate Dungeon (Level 200+), and Advanced Dungeon (Level 260+)
    • New Advanced Dungeon maps include Cernium, Hotel Arcus, and Odium
    • Removal of certain Beginner Dungeon maps
    • Additional 20 Monster Park coins given as daily reward for first daily clearance
    • New Bounty Hunter Monsterblooms sold in the Monster Park shop, costing 150 coins each up to 5 per week per world
  • Boss battle improvements and changes
    • Cooldown reset for most skills, full soul charge for Soul Weapons, and full combo / gauge for certain classes when entering any weekly/monthly bosses
      • Cooldown reset for most skills also applied when entering Sharenian Culvert and Golden Tomato Punch King
    • Removal of buffs upon entering boss lobbies, where players can use all buffs and skills after entering the lobbies
      • Most buffs no longer removed when entering Sharenian Culvert and Golden Tomato Punch King
    • Debuffs to bosses now visible under the HP bar
    • Cross World Boss Content via Dimensional Battleground expanded to Hard Lotus and above, with level requirement lowered to Level 200+
    • Removal of Fragment of Destiny requirement for Black Mage as well as prerequisite items (Spark of Determination and Shadow of Annihilation)
    • Boss adjustments and pattern improvements for Hard Lucid, Chosen Seren, Kaling, and Baldrix
    • Immediate entry to boss battle map for Normal Root Abyss bosses and Hilla
      • New map added in Azwan to include Bloodfang and Pikeman Skeleknight
    • Sayram's Elixir and Guild Skills duration in Boss Practice Mode and Fog Forest Training Grounds reduced to 15 and 30 minutes respectively
  • Genesis Weapon Liberation changes
    • Duo party made available Lotus and Damien liberation missions, with final damage reduced by 50% per party member
    • Maximum amount of Traces of Darkness that can be held increased to 3,000
    • New Genesis Pass, costing 30,000 NX purchasable once per Nexon ID
      • Triple amount of Traces of Darkness obtained, reducing liberation time to as low as 4 weeks
      • Liberation missions can be challenged with up to 6 players (all players will need to have the Pass), with final damage reduced by 50% per party member
      • Temporary stat buff provided when fighting bosses that grant Traces of Darkness
      • A box guaranteeing a 3% ATT/MATT option from a Commander Boss Soul received upon successful liberation
  • Cash improvements
    • Unification of Cash Storage, allowing all characters to access the same inventory in the same world
    • New Freestyle Coupon, removing gender requirements for cash items including hair and face
    • Reduction of Beauty Mix Coupon prices
    • Maple Rewards held displayed at the top of the Cash Shop screen at all times
  • Express Pass improvements
    • Experience awarded when using Symbol Express Pass on Arcane River Special Content
    • Achievements counted and awarded when using Symbol Express Pass on Arcane River Special Content and Epic Dungeon Express Pass
    • Epic Dungeon Express Pass can be used even if the character does not have a clear record
  • UI revamps, such as storage system access from inventory including Boss Rewards tab from Cross World Boss Content
  • Unification of weekly content reset on Thursdays, including Arcane River Special Content, Mu Lung Dojo, Tower of Oz, Ghost Park, weekly quests, and Legion coin shop
  • New Ariant and Aqua Road quests
  • Hyper Burning MAX, Beyond Burning, Tera Blink, and Item Burning events
    • 1+4 level ups to Level 260 for Hyper Burning MAX, and Ren can be assigned as a Hyper Burning MAX character
    • 1+1 level ups from Level 260 to 270 for Beyond Burning, and existing Level 260 to 268 characters can participate
    • Additional rewards provided for reaching certain level milestones with Burning Beyond, such as Sol Erda, Sol Erda Fragments and Sacred Symbols
  • Jump-Start Tutorial events
    • Character Creation Guide event, which serves as an introduction for new users up to Level 260 without any rewards
    • Tutorial Mission event, exclusive for Hyper Burning MAX characters, similar to the Winter Tutorial Mission in the previous NEXT Update
  • Challenger World Season 2
    • More tiers added, with level missions expanded to Level 285, and boss missions expanded to include up to Easy Kaling
    • Increased world stats when reaching certain tiers, with more rewards available
    • New Arcane Seal item, offering additional stats exclusive to Challenger World, enhanced with success rates similar to Star Force Enhancements
    • Updated Challenger World Coin Shop with additional items, including a separate list of items obtainable via clearing harder boss missions
    • New Challenger Pass providing rewards to support growth via completing weekly missions, with additional paid pass costing 19,800 Maple Points providing additional rewards and buffs
  • New Job Ren events including Wanderer's Travelogue, Tracks of the Wanderer, and Plum Blossoms Challenging the Sun
    • First 10 Ren characters in each Challenger World defeating Normal Chosen Seren will receive 5 million Maple Points
  • Twilight Eve Festival event
    • Event lore related to Chronica, the Transcendent of Time of Grandis
    • Includes weekly collection goal events where gauge is filled when all players participate in the event, offering new temporary event stats including increased base EXP for Epic Dungeons
  • New Name Auction event

2nd: Ascent Skills

  • 2nd HEXA Skill Node, Ascent Skill for every class
    • Can be used up to 3 times without cooldown during boss battles, with a 240-second cooldown applied in other maps
    • Invincible while skill is being used
    • Can only be used when boss with highest max HP is available to be attacked
    • Does not activate additional hits or effects that activate when direct attacks hit an enemy
    • Skills prioritize attacking boss with the highest max HP, with damage dealt even if enemy uses Damage Reflect or Damage Ignore
    • Ignore Enemy Defense and Boss Damage effects applied additively to base skill effect
    • Damage output not affected by stat increases from the following effects:
      • Hat and Rings
      • Passive skills effects that activate under certain conditions as well as active skill use effects
      • Monsters' elemental properties, patterns and debuffs
      • Consumables and cash items with duration of less than 30 minutes
  • Skill changes for various classes, including Ren
  • Sharenian Culvert changes
    • Maximum number of stages increased to 7, with increased HP, defense and damage required to obtain 1 point
    • Maximum points acquired in Stage 6 changed to 300,000 points
    • New Sharenian Culvert achievement
  • Treasure Hunter improvements, including guaranteeing a Treasure after defeating certain number of monsters
  • Guardian Angel Slime, Kaling, and Baldrix boss pattern changes
  • Odium field map improvements
  • UI improvements
    • Shop UI revamp, with new Expand UI feature
    • Further UI improvements in Equipment, Meso Market, and Maple Auction notifications
  • Ren's story added to Grand Athenaeum
  • Nexon Link Beta feature, allowing remote play of client from mobile devices
    • Requires Nexon Link on mobile and Nexon Plug on PC
    • Certain limitations apply, including maximum of 10,000 monsters per day via mobile
  • Simplified Event Start feature made available even if events were not previously started across Maple ID
  • Gold Richie’s Secret Safe event
  • Sayaroid Giveaway! event

3rd: The First Adversary and Skill Balancing I

  • New Boss: The First Adversary (Easy to Extreme)
    • Takes place in Odium, and is a Level 270+ Boss for up to 3 party members
  • New Equipment: Immortal Legacy (third piece of the Brilliant Boss Accessory Set)
    • Level 250+ medal with Bonus Stats and Exceptional Enhancement available to be applied
  • Boss system reorganization
    • Maximum of 12 weekly bosses can be defeated per character per week
    • Entry to another weekly boss battle restricted when existing party weekly boss battle in progress
    • New boss rewards
      • First Adversary's Soul Shard from the First Adversary (Easy to Extreme)
      • Adversaryroid from the First Adversary (Normal to Extreme)
      • Whisper of Ancient Resolve from Normal First Adversary
      • Echo of Ancient Resolve and Ancient Eternal Armor Box (Eternal Hat, Top, Bottom, or Shoulder) from the First Adversary (Hard and Extreme)
      • Exceptional Hammer (Medal) from Extreme First Adversary
      • Chaos Pitched Accessory Box from Kaling (Normal to Extreme), Limbo (Normal and Hard), and Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
        • Offers Pitched Boss Set items other than Genesis Badge, Mitra's Rage, and Total Control, at a set probability
        • Drop rate probability increases with higher difficulty bosses
    • Boss reward changes
      • Increased Reward Points from defeating Normal Seren and higher difficulty bosses
      • Increased Sol Erda Energy from defeating Limbo (Normal and Hard) and Baldrix (Normal and Hard)
      • Kalos's Residual Determination and Ferocious Beast Entanglement Ring no longer dropped separately per party member, with amount changes per defeat depending on the difficulty
      • Eternal Hats, Tops, Bottoms, and Shoulders from Kalos's Residual Determination, Ferocious Beast Entanglement Ring, Divine Eternal Armor Box, and Ferocious Beast Eternal Armor Box
      • Chaos Root Abyss medal requirements reduced to 1 kill count, with Root Abyss Master title awarded after obtaining all 4 medals
      • Hilla Specialist and Savior of the Empress medal requirements reduced to 1 kill count of Hard Hilla and Cygnus (Easy or Normal) respectively
      • Guild contribution changed to 35,000 points per week, with Hard Lotus and higher difficulty bosses increased to 3,000 points per boss defeat
      • Boss reward replacements
        • Kalos's Residual Determination Fragment from Normal Kalos, replacing the Kalos's Residual Determination item drop
        • Ferocious Entanglement Ring Fragment from Normal Kaling, replacing the Ferocious Beast Entanglement Ring item drop
        • Grindstone of Faith from Kaling (Hard and Extreme), replacing the Grindstone of Life item drop
      • Drop rate changes
        • Pitched Boss Set drop rate reduction from defeating Hard Verus Hilla and lower difficulty bosses' drop tables
        • Grindstone of Life drop rate reduction from Normal Kalos's drop table
        • Removal of Damaged Black Heart from Lotus's (Hard and Extreme) drop tables
        • Removal of Kalos's Residual Determination Fragment from Easy Kalos's drop table
  • Part 1 of skill balancing for various classes, with Parts 2 and 3 taking place in the Fall 2025 Update
    • New temporary final damage increase skill added to classes not affected by this part of skill balancing
  • New Swap Rings skill, offering instant Special Skill Ring swaps
    • Cooldown set at 10 seconds, and is not affected by any cooldown resets or cooldown reduction effects
    • New Special Skill Ring spare slot in Equipment UI
  • UI revamps and improvements for Party, Friends and Chat
    • Increased maximum number of friends in Friends List to 200
    • New Friend Search, Friend Request List, and Away status features
    • Combat Power and login status display made available in Party UI
    • Friend and party member's location made available to be checked even in different channels
    • New chat window transparency option
  • Item improvements
    • Genesis and Destiny Weapons can no longer be discarded
    • Destiny Weapon Legendary Boss Monster Damage line increased to +35%
    • Increased maximum items per inventory slot for certain items
      • Power Elixirs, Reindeer Milk, Twilight Dew, All-Cure Potion: 99,999 in one slot
      • Spell Traces and Pet Food: 90,000 in one slot
  • Maple Home improvements
    • New Spiegelmann Furniture Shop
    • New furniture rewards added to certain Maple Achievements
  • Challenger World Mission event changes, including inclusion of the First Adversary and increased purchase limits for Karma Abyssal Rebirth Flames
  • Tera Blink made available for all Hyper Burning characters regardless of event period
  • Undying Purpose event
  • Frontier Pass event

Various quality of life improvements introduced in the first two parts KoreaMS Assemble Update are rolled out in the first part of the update. In addition, the update also includes:

  • New Job: Ren, the Red-Eyed Wanderer, a Rabbit Anima Warrior
  • New Area: Vallora, the starting area for Ren
  • Star Force Enhancement reorganization, previously introduced in other servers in the NEXT / Post-NEXT Update
    • Higher Star Force level recovered when restoring high Star Force level items during enhancement
  • Princess No boss revamp
    • Prequest completion no longer required
    • Removal of all previous stages, with minibosses changed to be summoned by Princess No for a set duration
    • Increase in difficulty with higher HP
  • Akechi Mitsuhide boss difficulty increase with higher HP per phase
  • Mushroom Shrine map revamp
  • Erda's Request converted into a weekly quest, providing 9 Sol Erdas and 90 Sol Erda Fragments per account per week
  • New Swap Rings skill, offering instant Special Skill Ring swaps
  • Exclusive quality of life improvements
    • Expansion of Level 3 Link Skills to all jobs
    • New systems to obtain and restore equipment, including:
      • New Whisper Crystals from defeating certain bosses in exchange for Dawn Boss Set or Pitched Boss Set items including Mithra's Rage and Genesis Badge
      • Restoring equipment destroyed by Star Force Enhancement through specific boss missions, as well as Star Specks collected from hunting
    • Familiar System improvements
      • UI revamp, including permanently unlocked Presets
      • Inventory stack limit increased to 9,999, with new Familiar Card Bag available to be purchased
      • Removal and adjustment of Familiar Potential and stats
      • New Rank-Up Guarantee system, offering guaranteed Rank-Ups in tiers up to Unique
    • Temporary Sacred Power buff provided when entering Cernium at 30 Sacred Power or lower
    • Transfer of AbsoLab and Arcane Umbra boxes within the account made available in Heroic Worlds
    • Implementation of Boss Reward Distribution System in Heroic Worlds
    • Removal of Hyper Stats presets changing costs
    • Events can be added as favorites
    • Multiple Monster Park tickets purchasable at once
    • Sales price can be changed when relisting items on the Auction House
    • Maple Tour Express Pass Perk added to MVP Red and Black ranks
    • Inventory bag limit increase
      • Up to 10 bags for herb, mineral and production bags
      • Up to 2 bags for all other bags
    • New Skin tab in Beauty Salon UI, where skin colors can now be saved
  • Wisp's Wonderberry items now ongoing sales, where items are perpetually sold until new item releases
  • New Genesis Pass, costing 30,000 NX purchasable twice per Nexon ID
  • Hyper Burning MAX, Beyond Burning, Tera Blink, and Item Burning events
    • New Ignitia set effect lasting for the Item Burning duration, available to be applied on any weapon and armor slot
  • Jump-Start Tutorial event
  • Challenger World Season 2, including Challenger Pass and Arcane Seal item
  • Arthur’s Equipment Rental event, providing short-term rentals of specific equipment, costing meso for first rental and Maple Points after the first rental
  • New Job Ren events including Wanderer's Travelogue and Tracks of the Wanderer
  • Get a Sayaroid event
  • Frontier Pass event
  • Twilight Eve Festival event

2nd: Ascent Skills and The First Adversary

  • 2nd HEXA Skill Node, Ascent Skill for all classes originated from KoreaMS
    • Kanna, Hayato, Lynn, Mo Xuan, and Sia Astelle have their Ascent Skills delayed to the post-update
  • New Mystic Frontier system, an expansion of the Familiar System
    • Send Familiars and depart on exploration missions to receive various rewards
    • Rewards include Chaos Pitched Accessory Box, Resonant Dawn Accessory Box, Karma Bright Cubes, Roro Nodestone, and Roro's Rings
    • Roro's Rings offer Special Skills catered to mobbing, while Roro Nodestone works similar to mobbing-related Mapae Nodestones from Ride or Die event
  • New Equipment: Immortal Legacy (third piece of the Brilliant Boss Accessory Set)
    • Level 250+ medal with Bonus Stats and Exceptional Enhancement available to be applied
  • New boss rewards and changes introduced in KoreaMS, with certain exceptions
  • Trace Restoration System improvements
    • Boss mission progress message displayed for certain missions that require repetitive actions
    • Practice Mode now counts towards challenge completions
    • Extreme Mode added to boss missions
    • Point adjustments for Eternal and Brilliant Sets, with maximum points accumulated increased to 2,500 points
    • Gauge points transferred when leaping from Challenger Worlds to Heroic or Interactive Worlds, with maximum of 2,500 points transferred
  • Windows 11 Efficiency Mode setting
  • Ride or Die Season 3 exclusive event
    • Rewards include Libae's Ring Box, Inspector's Grindstone, as well as Chains of Resentment (Interactive Worlds only), and an additional Event Legion Block
    • Libae's Ring Box offers temporary Special Skill Rings, while Inspector's Grindstone enhances these rings up to Level 6
  • Undying Purpose event
  • Twenteena's Adventure Day exclusive event
  • [Home] Maplemas event, including Maplemas Meso Shop

The following changes from KoreaMS are not implemented:

  • 12 weekly bosses defeat limit
  • Pitched Boss Set drop rate reduction from defeating Hard Verus Hilla and lower difficulty bosses' drop tables
  • Grindstone of Life drop rate reduction from Normal Kalos's drop table
  • Removal of Damaged Black Heart from Lotus's (Hard and Extreme) drop tables

Trivia

  • Server Naming Difference
    • GMS: Every Little Thing Every Precious Thing
    • MSEA/JMS/TMS: Assemble
    • CMS: 莲合行动 (Lián hé xíngdòng)
  • As of this update, Flying / Anti-AFK monsters are instanced, regardless of the map is instanced or not.
  • A number of regional differences came across with this update:
    • In Non-KoreaMS servers, the following differences apply:
      • Nexon Link Beta feature was not implemented, as this app was only available in KoreaMS.
      • Plum Blossoms Challenging the Sun, New Name Auction, and Gold Richie’s Secret Safe events were not available.
      • The skill balancing introduced in the third part of the update was delayed to the post-update.
      • Unification of Cash Inventory was handled differently depending on the servers:
        • In Non-GlobalMS servers, this was made available upon rollout, similar to KoreaMS.
          • In ChinaMS, the maximum number of slots is limited to 40,000.
          • In JapanMS, this was already available before this update.
        • In GlobalMS, this was not available upon rollout, and remains unavailable as of now.
          • GlobalMS intends on having more frequent Cash Inventory Transfer events in lieu of the unification of Cash Inventory, beginning in the Summer 2026 Update.[19]
      • In GlobalMS and MapleSEA, Wisp's Wonderberry and Luna Crystals are made perpetually available to be purchased.
        • This was previously already implemented in KoreaMS in the Post-New Age Update.
    • In JapanMS, MacOS support was also announced during the Assemble showcase, with release scheduled in 2026.
    • In GlobalMS, the following differences apply:
      • Home furniture from various events were excluded and replaced with other reward items.
      • MacOS beta client was also launched in this update.[20]
